Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Han dynasty palace scene. Elderly Wang Zhengjun as Grand Empress Dowager on a throne, numerous Wang family officials surrounding her like a wall, a shadowy figure of Wang Mang in the background looming over the Han imperial seal. The mood is ominous despite the grandeur. Chinese title '"王氏专权"' in calligraphic brush style.
