Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Su Wu returning to Chang'an in 81 BC — an old man with white hair and beard, holding a bare staff (all tassels gone), kneeling at the city gate. Nineteen years of exile finally over. Ancient Chinese man in worn Han clothing. Chinese title "归汉" in calligraphic brush style. Subtitle "白发节".
