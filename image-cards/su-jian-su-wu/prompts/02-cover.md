Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Su Jian under Wei Qing's command supervising the construction of Shuofang city — rammed earth walls rising, Han soldiers working, a fortress being built at the foot of the Yinshan Mountains. Ancient Chinese soldiers in Han armor. Chinese title "苏建" in calligraphic brush style. Subtitle "筑朔方".
