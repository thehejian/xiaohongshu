Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Two scenes side by side: Su Jian directing the construction of Shuofang city wall with Han soldiers, and Su Wu tending sheep by frozen Lake Baikal holding his Han staff. Father and son — two kinds of loyalty. Ancient Chinese people in Han period clothing. Chinese title "苏建苏武" in calligraphic brush style.
