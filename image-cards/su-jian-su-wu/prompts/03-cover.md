Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Su Jian and Zhao Xin with only 3,000 Han cavalry facing tens of thousands of Xiongnu warriors on the battlefield. After fierce fighting Zhao Xin surrenders, Su Jian escapes alone. A scene of desperate battle and defeat. Ancient Chinese soldiers in Han armor. Chinese title "败赎" in calligraphic brush style. Subtitle "为庶人".
