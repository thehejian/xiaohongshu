Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Su Wu herding sheep by frozen Lake Baikal (the North Sea), holding his Han diplomatic staff with most of its tassels fallen off. Snow-covered landscape, vast and desolate. Ancient Chinese man in worn Han clothing. Chinese title "北海" in calligraphic brush style. Subtitle "十九年".
