Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Su Wu in a Xiongnu tent after stabbing himself in a desperate act of defiance, refusing to surrender. Han attendants rushing to save him. His face shows pain but unwavering resolve. Ancient Chinese people in Han and Xiongnu clothing. Chinese title "不屈" in calligraphic brush style. Subtitle "引刀刺".
