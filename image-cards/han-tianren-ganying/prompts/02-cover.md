Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Han dynasty courtyard. A young boy (the future official Zhang Tang) sternly presiding over an impromptu miniature court, judging a rat caught stealing meat; a bound rat on a table before him, his father watching in astonishment. The boy writes a formal verdict like an experienced judge. Chinese title '"小儿审鼠"' in calligraphic brush style.
