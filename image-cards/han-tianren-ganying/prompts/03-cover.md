Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Night sky. A lone brilliant red star (Mars / 'yinghuo') blazing above the Han palace. Below, a chancellor in court robes kneels receiving an imperial edict, his head bowed in despair, attendants holding lanterns in the dark. The ominous omen blamed on one official's conduct. Chinese title '"荧惑守心"' in calligraphic brush style. No blood, no violence.
