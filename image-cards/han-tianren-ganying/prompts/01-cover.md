Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. A Han dynasty emperor kneeling at his writing desk drafting a self-criticism edict, brush in hand, expression solemn and weary. Outside the window, a dark swarm of locusts blots out the sky. The emperor's responsibility under the heaven-mandate warning system. Chinese title '"罪己诏·蝗灾"' in calligraphic brush style.
