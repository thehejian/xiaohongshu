Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Ancient Chinese people, Sang Hongyang in Han official robes, merchant convoy in civilian Han clothing. Chinese title "盐铁官营·均输平准" in calligraphic brush style. No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
