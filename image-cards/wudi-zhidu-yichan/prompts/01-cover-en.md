Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Left side: a Qin dynasty administrative foundation built of brick and stone, structured and bare. Right side: Han Emperor Wu fills the foundation with elements of governance—bamboo scrolls (Confucian thought), strings of Wuzhu coins, and an imperial tally (Cishi symbol). A complete city wall and watchtower takes shape. Metaphor of Qin skeleton and Han flesh completing a system that lasted two millennia. Ancient Chinese people, Qin official clothing and Han dynasty official clothing side by side. Chinese title "秦砖汉瓦·百代皆行" in calligraphic brush style. No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
