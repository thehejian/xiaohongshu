Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. A Cishi (imperial inspector) with a mere 600-shi salary rides on horseback holding an imperial tally, patrolling the 13 provinces. Below him: commandery governors in high-rank robes sit nervously in their offices, their wealth and power under watch. A web of imperial surveillance spreads across the land—the eye of the emperor everywhere. Ancient Chinese people, Cishi in low-rank Han official robes with tally, governor in high-rank ornate robes. Chinese title "六百石监察二千石" in calligraphic brush style. No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
