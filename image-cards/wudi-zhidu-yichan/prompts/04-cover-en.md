Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. A humble scholar holding bamboo scrolls climbs stone steps upward. Above him hang golden plaques reading "Xiaolian" (Filial and Incorrupt) and "Maocai" (Excellent Talent). On the right, an aristocratic family's gate closes gradually—their hereditary path fading. A new pathway from the fields to the court opens. The first nationwide recommendation-based official selection system in Chinese history. Ancient Chinese people, humble scholar in plain hemp Confucian robes, aristocrats in ornate clothing.
