Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Han Emperor Wu in dark ritual robes and crown stands before a large wooden plaque inscribed "建元" (Jianyuan)—the first reign title in Chinese history. Beneath it, a long bamboo scroll unfurls leftward carrying classical Chinese text representing the Taichu calendar reform. The scroll transforms into a misty river of ink flowing through time, with faint era names floating in the distance like "令和" and other East Asian reign titles. A quiet, philosophical scene about the gift of timekeeping spanning two millennia. Ancient Chinese people, Emperor Wu in Han ritual robes. Chinese title "年号与太初历" in calligraphic brush style. No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
