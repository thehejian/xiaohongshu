Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. A Han dynasty marketplace scene with two parallel activities — a government official inspecting coins at a stall while a clandestine forger in a dim workshop secretly casting thin, lightweight imitation coins. Contrast between official mint quality and crude counterfeits. Chinese title "盗铸·博弈" in calligraphic brush style, subtitle "国抽钱·民偷铸". No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
