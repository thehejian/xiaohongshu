Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, abundant negative space, misty atmosphere. Chen Ping at a lavish banquet in his residence — robe half-open, lounging sideways on a mat, wine cup raised, eyes drunken and unfocused, with female musicians playing beside him. The scene exudes decadence and dissipation. But look at his eyes: beneath the drunken haze, a barely detectable flash of sharp alertness, secretly observing everything. Outside the window, the faint silhouette of Empress Lü's spy lurks. Chinese title: 装醉. Subtitle: 吕后以为他是废物. Ancient Chinese people, Western Han, Chen Ping in casual drunken robe, female musicians.
