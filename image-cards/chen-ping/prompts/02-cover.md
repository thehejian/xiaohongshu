Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, abundant negative space, misty atmosphere. Inside Liu Bang's military tent, tense atmosphere. Zhou Bo and Guan Ying stand to the side pointing angrily at Chen Ping. Liu Bang sits at his desk with a grave expression. Chen Ping kneels in the tent, calm and composed, hands spread open as if saying "this is who I am." On the desk before him lie several pieces of gold — the bribes he was accused of taking. The scene captures the moment he said: "I came empty-handed; I needed the money to survive. Use my schemes or return the gold, I'll leave." Chinese title: 盗嫂受金. Subtitle: 我不辩解，你看着用. Ancient Chinese people, Western Han, Liu Bang in emperor's informal robe, Chen Ping in strategist's plain robe, Zhou Bo and Guan Ying in military armor. No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
