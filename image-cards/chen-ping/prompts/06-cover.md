Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, abundant negative space, misty atmosphere. Grand panoramic view — Chen Ping standing on high ground overlooking the land, wearing Western Han chancellor court robe (plain black, NO dragon patterns), robes billowing in the wind. In the distant lower part of the composition, a magnificent landscape unfolds: fields, cities, envoy caravans from the four directions coming to pay tribute. Flowing ink strokes of yin and yang energy swirl in the sky above — representing his famous answer about the chancellor's duty. But in one corner, his shadow cast on the wall reveals a different posture — the silhouette of a schemer, contrasting with the dignified chancellor's image before him. Chinese title: 上佐天子理阴阳. Subtitle: 盗嫂小人，与调和阴阳的宰相. Ancient Chinese people, Western Han chancellor in plain black court robe, NO dragon patterns. No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
