Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, abundant negative space, misty atmosphere. Late night secret meeting. Chen Ping and Zhou Bo sit facing each other in a dim room, a candle between them, its flame illuminating two very different faces — Zhou Bo (military general) with resolute fierce determination, hand on sword; Chen Ping (strategist) with calm expression, fingers drawing a layout plan on the desk as he whispers his explanation. Between them on the table lies a silk scroll inscribed "诛诸吕" (Exterminate the Lü clan). The scene is filled with an air of impending violence. Chinese title: 一夜诛诸吕. Subtitle: 装废物的，才是全场最清醒的人. Ancient Chinese people, Western Han, Zhou Bo in armor, Chen Ping in strategist robe. Candlelight illumination. No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
