Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Han dynasty Weiyang Palace on New Year's Day. Emperor Xuan sitting sternly on the throne surrounded by piles of bamboo scrolls, interrogating nervous officials one by one. Outside the palace gates, families celebrate the new year with fireworks and feasts, but inside it is judgment day. Chinese title '"正旦考绩·地狱模式"' in calligraphic brush style.
