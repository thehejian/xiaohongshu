Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Han dynasty New Year scene. Local officials ushering prisoners out of the prison gates with bundles of belongings, sending them home for New Year — while a clerk holds a brush ready to write "prison empty" on the official report. In the distance, the same prisoners are shown being rounded up and brought back after New Year. Chinese title '"狱空率·数据注水"' in calligraphic brush style.
