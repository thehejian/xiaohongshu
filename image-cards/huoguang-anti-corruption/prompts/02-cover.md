Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, vivid crimson and grey colours, dramatic composition, misty atmosphere. A dramatic raid scene at a grand Han dynasty mansion: imperial guards and御史 in black uniforms burst through the gates, carrying bamboo chests and scrolls, discovering piles of gold ingots, rolls of silk, and forbidden weapons hidden in a secret chamber. A defiant-looking official in fine robes (Sang Hongyang) stands with head held high while his son Sang Qian cowers behind him, surrounded by guards. Opulent furnishings, silk curtains, the exposure of immense hidden wealth, torchlight illuminating the scene, dramatic tension. Chinese title "查抄府邸" in large bold calligraphic brush style.
