Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, vivid crimson and grey colours, dramatic composition, misty atmosphere. A dramatic scene inside the Han imperial court: a court official reads aloud from a long bamboo scroll — the confiscated property list of the fallen minister Sang Hongyang — while stunned ministers in dark robes whisper and exchange shocked glances. The list includes gold, land deeds,商铺 titles, and forbidden weapons. The empty seat of Sang Hongyang in the court hall serves as a silent warning. The moment of utter financial ruin and political destruction, the downfall of a once-powerful minister, candlelight, tension, the weight of imperial justice. Chinese title "宣读清单" in large bold calligraphic brush style.
