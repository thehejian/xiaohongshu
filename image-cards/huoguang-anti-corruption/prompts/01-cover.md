Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, vivid crimson and grey colors, dramatic composition, misty atmosphere. A dramatic scene inside the Han court: the stern-faced Grand General Huo Guang in dark official robes sits on a raised dais, a bamboo scroll decree in hand, his gaze cold and commanding. Before him kneel trembling officials in court robes, their faces pale with fear. Armed御史 (imperial censors) in black uniforms stand at the sides holding bamboo slips and scrolls — evidence of corruption. The tension of a sweeping anti-corruption campaign, the power of the regent bearing down on corrupt officials, candlelight and shadow. Chinese title "霍光肃贪" in large bold calligraphic brush style.
