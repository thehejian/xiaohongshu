Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, vivid crimson and grey colors, dramatic composition, misty atmosphere. A dramatic scene at a well in a Han dynasty county: a local official (Meng Tong) in muddy robes kneels beside a newly dug well, pointing in astonishment at a large white stone with a rounded top and flat bottom, freshly unearthed. On the stone's surface, eight vivid red characters are clearly visible. Workers with digging tools gather around, murmuring and pointing. Dusty earth, ropes and buckets, the moment of discovering a "heaven-sent" omen that would change history. Chinese title "石告符" in large bold calligraphic brush style.
