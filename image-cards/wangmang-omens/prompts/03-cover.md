Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, vivid crimson and grey colors, dramatic composition, misty atmosphere. A solemn, theatrical scene at the Han dynasty Gaozu Temple (Gaomiao): a mysterious figure in yellow robes (Ai Zhang) kneels reverently before the temple altar, holding up a golden casket (jinkui) with both hands, his face illuminated by candlelight. Behind the altar, the spirit tablets of Han emperors stand in silent rows, incense smoke curling upward. Temple guards in ceremonial armor watch in awe. The dramatic moment of a fabricated "heavenly mandate" being delivered to the temple of the founder, a staged but convincing performance, ancient mystery. Chinese title "金匮策书" in large bold calligraphic brush style.
