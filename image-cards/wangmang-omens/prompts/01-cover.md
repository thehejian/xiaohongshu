Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, vivid crimson and grey colors, dramatic composition, misty atmosphere. A dramatic scene inside the Han dynasty court: rows of officials in dark court robes present a bizarre collection of auspicious omens before the throne — a rough white stone with vivid red characters carved into it, an ancient turtle shell etched with mysterious markings, a painted silk banner showing a yellow dragon coiling through clouds. A court eunuch holds up the stone for all to see. The atmosphere is tense, theatrical, half-believed and half-staged, candlelight flickering, the grand hall of Weiyang Palace looming behind. Chinese title "祥瑞造假" in large bold calligraphic brush style.
