Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Inside a beast enclosure, Confucian scholar Yuansheng holds a sharp blade and pierces a wild boar straight through the heart—the boar collapses. Outside the enclosure, Empress Dowager Dou in phoenix crown and formal robes sits watching coldly, while Emperor Jing beside her sweats in secret tension. Intense, dramatic composition. Ancient Chinese people, Han dynasty empress dowager, emperor and scholar clothing. Chinese title "辕固刺豕" in calligraphic brush style. No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
