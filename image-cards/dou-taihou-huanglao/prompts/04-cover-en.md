Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Chancellor Cao Can drinks and sings freely in his residence, at ease. Outside the window, farmers peacefully till the fields amidst rising cooking smoke. In the distance, grain piles tower at the imperial granary and coin strings scatter. Warm, abundant composition symbolizing the fruits of Huang-Lao governance. Ancient Chinese people, Han dynasty chancellor and farmer clothing. Chinese title "清静而民自定" in calligraphic brush style. No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
