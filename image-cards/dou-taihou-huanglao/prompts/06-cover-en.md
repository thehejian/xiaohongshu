Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. In the sixth year of Jianyuan (135 BC), Empress Dowager Dou passes away at Changle Palace—solemn funeral ceremony. On the other side of the scene, Emperor Wu stands alone in the court hall, unfurling Dong Zhongshu's Confucian memorial as dawn light streams through the window. Huang-Lao texts gather dust on the desk. Composition shifts from oppressive to open, marking a historic turning point. Ancient Chinese people, Han dynasty emperor and scholar clothing. Chinese title "独尊儒术" in calligraphic brush style. No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
