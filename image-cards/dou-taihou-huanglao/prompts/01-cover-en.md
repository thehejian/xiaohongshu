Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Left half: young Dou Yifang as a palace maid holding an oar at a waterside pavilion, graceful and pure. Right half: elderly her as Grand Empress Dowager in phoenix crown and formal robes seated majestically on a high platform. A palace-wall cloud pattern divides the two halves, symbolizing her journey from maid to power. Ancient Chinese people, Han dynasty palace maid and empress dowager clothing. Chinese title "窦太皇太后" in calligraphic brush style. No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
