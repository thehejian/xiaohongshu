Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. A Han dynasty man in official robes (朱买臣) standing at the gate of a manor, pouring a basin of water onto the ground, a former wife in simple clothing kneeling before him with a pleading expression. An ox-cart and servants behind him. Autumn leaves falling. Chinese title "覆水难收" in calligraphic brush style, subtitle "泼水·难复". No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
