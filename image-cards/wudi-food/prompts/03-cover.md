Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. A Han dynasty relay station (驿站) scene: a rider dismounts in front of a small station building, handing a bamboo tube with lychees to a fresh rider on a waiting horse. A relay tower (堠) marks the distance. Dust rises from the road. The atmosphere is urgent and relentless. Chinese title "荔枝驿·七日到" in calligraphic brush style, subtitle "岭南鲜果·长安殿前". No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
