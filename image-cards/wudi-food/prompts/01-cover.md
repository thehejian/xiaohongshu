Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. A Han dynasty scene showing a relay road stretching across mountains and plains, with a lone rider on horseback racing through mist carrying a bamboo tube. In the foreground, an exhausted horse lies on the ground. A pavilion-style relay station (驿站) appears in the distance. Chinese title "武帝·荔枝驿" in calligraphic brush style, subtitle "十里一置·五里一堠". No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
