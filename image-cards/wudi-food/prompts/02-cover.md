Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. A Han dynasty glass-walled greenhouse palace building (扶荔宫) where southern fruit trees - lychee, longan, citrus - are planted in large ceramic pots under careful cultivation. Gardeners tend to the struggling trees. The northern landscape outside the windows contrasts with the tropical plants inside. Chinese title "扶荔宫·南植北" in calligraphic brush style, subtitle "连年不活·温室移栽". No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
