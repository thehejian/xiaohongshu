Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Han dynasty shenyi robe with crossed-collar right closure, traditional topknot with guan headpiece, prison interior with wooden bars and straw. Bing Ji in official robe secretly handing a bowl of food to a toddler through prison bars, a humble oil lamp beside him. The contrast between his dignified official robe and the prison setting creates poignancy. Chinese title '"狱中哺养五年"' in calligraphic brush style.
