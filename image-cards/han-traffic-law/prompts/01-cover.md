Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Busy Chang'an street scene, Han dynasty. A luxurious carriage racing through a crowded market, scattering vendors and pedestrians, dust flying, chaos and congestion. The traffic nightmare of the ancient capital. Chinese title '"长安飙车"' in calligraphic brush style.
