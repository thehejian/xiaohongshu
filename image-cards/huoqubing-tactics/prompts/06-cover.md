Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Huo Qubing's funeral procession: the Xiongnu tribal soldiers he conquered (shuguo army) lining up in black armor from Chang'an to Maoling. The Qilian-shaped tomb mound stands in the distance. Xiongnu soldiers in Han-style black lamellar armor and helmets bowing their heads in grief — their facial features still bearing Xiongnu characteristics. They mourn the man who defeated them and won their respect. Chinese title "属国送葬·祁连长望" in calligraphic brush style. No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
