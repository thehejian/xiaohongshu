Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Huo Qubing's forces raiding a Xiongnu royal camp to "live off the enemy": Han cavalry attacking a Xiongnu tribal settlement, slaughtering sheep and cattle for food, taking horses to replace losses. Xiongnu tents and livestock being seized. Desert wilderness in the distance. Han soldiers in small lamellar armor, riding trousers, with ring-pommel swords, capturing livestock. Xiongnu herdsmen fleeing in the distance, wearing left-lapel Hu-style fur robes (hufu), braided hair, barefoot. Chinese title "取食于敌" in calligraphic brush style. No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
