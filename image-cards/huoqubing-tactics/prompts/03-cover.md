Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. War council inside Huo Qubing's command tent: surrendered Xiongnu generals Gao Bushi (former Xiongnu Ju Prince), Pu Duo (Xiongnu tribal king), Zhao Ponu (Han commander who grew up among Xiongnu), Fu Luzhi (former Xiongnu Yinchun Prince), Yi Jijian (former Xiongnu Louzhuan Prince) standing in a circle pointing at maps of the northern desert (Mobei). Tense and confident atmosphere. Huo Qubing in black lamellar armor and military cap seated at the head. Surrendered Xiongnu generals in left-lapel fur robes, sable fur caps, braided hair — contrasted with Han-style cross-collar right-over-left robes. Chinese title "匈奴参谋团" in calligraphic brush style. No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
