Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Split composition: left side General Li Guang lost and wandering in the desert alone while the Chanyu escapes in the distance; right side Huo Qubing with surrendered Xiongnu guides precisely tracking the Xiongnu main force. Surrendered Xiongnu soldiers leading the way, Han cavalry following, the Xiongnu camp exposed beyond the dunes. Left: Li Guang in Han general's black lamellar armor and military cap, confused and isolated. Right: Huo Qubing in black lamellar armor on horseback; Xiongnu guides in left-lapel fur robes with braided hair pointing the way ahead; Han cavalry in small lamellar armor and helmets following. Chinese title "大漠不迷向" in calligraphic brush style. No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
