Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Huo Qubing leading light cavalry in lightning march without supply wagons: each rider with two horses, no infantry or baggage trains, galloping across the desert like wind. In the distant background, a faint contrasting silhouette of Wei Qing-style supply convoy with long lines of grain-carrying laborers moving slowly. Fast versus slow side by side. Huo Qubing wears Han general's black lamellar armor (xuanjia), military cap with pheasant tail (wuguan), dark wrapped robe (shenyi) underneath, carrying a bow on horseback. Han cavalry in small lamellar armor (zhajia), riding trousers (ku-zhe), leather military caps (wubian), each with two horses. Chinese title "以战养战" in calligraphic brush style. No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
