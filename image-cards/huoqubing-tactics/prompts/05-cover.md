Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. The most dramatic moment of the Hexi surrender: Huo Qubing charging with several thousand cavalry straight into a camp of forty thousand Xiongnu, meeting King Hunxie face to face, then executing eight thousand rebels. Surrendered Xiongnu generals in his ranks calming the crowd, forty thousand tribesmen kneeling in submission. Dust rising, Han banners and Xiongnu banners intermingling. Huo Qubing in black lamellar armor and military cap charging on horseback. King Hunxie in left-lapel fur robe and sable cap greeting him. Xiongnu tribesmen in left-lapel fur robes, braided hair, kneeling in panic. Surrendered Xiongnu generals in traditional clothing calling out to calm the crowd. Chinese title "河西受降·四万归汉" in calligraphic brush style. No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
