Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Chaotic street scene outside Chang'an city gate: thousands of commoners pressing forward to see the old man in the yellow calf cart who claims to be the Crown Prince. Dust rises, the crowd buzzes with excitement and confusion. The old man sits in the cart with a smug expression, enjoying the attention. No officials dare approach. Chinese title '"万人围观"' in calligraphic brush style. No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
