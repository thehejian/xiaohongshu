Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. A mysterious cross-section of a Han dynasty underground tomb revealed like a cutaway diorama in the dim light of a lone oil lamp: overhead a thick, ominous layer of fine sand hangs suspended like a frozen tidal wave ready to break, above the burial chamber a silent network of ancient tunnels gapes open in shadow, and at the far end a massive cliff-tomb door sealed by colossal stone slabs looms in darkness. Faint dust motes drifting in a single shaft of pale moonlight piercing down through a crack. The scene breathes suspense and dread of what lies beneath the earth. Chinese title "汉墓防盗黑科技" in calligraphic brush style.
