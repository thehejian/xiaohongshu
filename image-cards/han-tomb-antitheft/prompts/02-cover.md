Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. A heart-stopping instant inside a Han dynasty tomb: a narrow robber's tunnel has just broken through the ceiling, and a roaring wall of dry sand explodes downward in a great funnel, a raging waterfall of golden grains flooding the shaft, dust swirling in a choking storm, the darkness below about to be buried in moments. A single clay oil lamp on the tunnel wall is caught mid-fall, its tiny flame almost drowned in the cascading sand, throwing wild dancing shadows. No visible people, no blood, no corpse. Pure raw motion, violence of gravity, breathless tension of a trap sprung. Chinese title "流沙·倾泻" in calligraphic brush style.
