Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. A forbidding Han dynasty cliff-tomb gate cut into raw mountain rock: rows of gigantic rectangular stone slabs stacked floor to ceiling, each seam fused with hardened iron that glints faintly, the stone surfaces scarred with chisel marks and ancient soot from forgotten torchlight. A single thin crack of pale light leaks through one seam, hinting at the dark chamber sealed beyond. Carved animal guardian motifs barely visible under moss. The overwhelming weight and silence of a door that has not opened for two thousand years. Chinese title "塞石·铁封" in calligraphic brush style.
