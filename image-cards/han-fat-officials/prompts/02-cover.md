Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Han dynasty imperial guard (yulinlang) annual military assessment on a training field. An overweight guard in Han uniform, belly protruding, running breathless behind a line of fit guards sprinting ahead, his arrow falling short of the target. A stern military officer writes a dismissal note. Contrast of the slow fat guard and the fit warriors. Chinese title '"肥胖·退伍"' in calligraphic brush style.
