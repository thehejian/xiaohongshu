Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Famine year in a Han dynasty county. An imperial inspector in an official cap and cross-collar robe examines a local official — plump, red-faced, well-fed — while gaunt starving villagers with hollow cheeks gather behind, thin as sticks. The inspector shakes his head and reaches for his bamboo scroll of dismissal. Contrast of the fat official and the starving people. Chinese title '"灾荒·肥官撤职"' in calligraphic brush style.
