Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. A Han dynasty imperial guard assessment ground. An overweight official in a cross-collar deep robe (jiaoling shenyi) sweating and panting, struggling to draw a heavy bow, his belly straining the robe; beside him a lean warrior in armor easily draws the bow to full moon. Officials record the results. The weight test that decides an official's fate. Chinese title '"羽林考核·因胖退伍"' in calligraphic brush style.
