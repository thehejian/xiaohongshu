Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. A Han dynasty scene split in two: on one side, a ritual with female shamans being exposed to the sun in a marketplace, city gates open and closed according to yin-yang theory; on the other side, a dark tomb passage with hidden crossbows mounted on walls, arrows ready to fire. Chinese title "武帝黑科技" in calligraphic brush style, subtitle "聚阳求雨·暗弩护陵". No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
