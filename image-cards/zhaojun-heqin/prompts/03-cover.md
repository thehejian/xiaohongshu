Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Northern grassland under grey sky. A single lush green grave mound standing alone on the vast yellow steppe where all grass has withered, a pipa lying on the tomb, wild geese flying south in the distance. The lonely beauty of Zhaojun's resting place. Chinese title '"青冢"' in calligraphic brush style.
