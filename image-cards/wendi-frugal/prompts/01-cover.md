Ink wash painting style on light rice paper, flowing ink strokes, sparse composition with generous negative space, subtle crimson and grey palette, warm candlelit mood. Interior of a grand Western Han palace hall with ornate rafters, but across the beams hang humble grey hemp messenger bags patched on top of patches, some bag mouths spilling yellowed bamboo-slip corners, draping down as makeshift curtains. Below, a Western Han emperor in plain black coarse silk robes with a flat-topped crown sits at a low desk lit by a single flickering candle, his face calm and frugal. Strong contrast between the shabby patched bags and the vast palace. Chinese calligraphic title inscription: "布袋子·当窗帘". No other text.
