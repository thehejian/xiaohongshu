Ink wash painting style on light rice paper, flowing ink strokes, sparse composition with generous negative space, subtle crimson and grey palette, misty atmosphere, consistent with the first panel. A palace courtyard: a Western Han emperor in plain dark robes and flat-topped crown stands with hands behind his back, firm expression, while a eunuch beside him holds up a tray of gleaming gold cakes which the emperor pushes away with one palm. In the foreground a crouching artisan clutches rolled blueprints, looking frustrated. Strong visual contrast between the gleaming gold and the emperor's plain robes. Chinese calligraphic title inscription: "百金·我不建". No other text.
