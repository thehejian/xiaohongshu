Ink wash painting style on light rice paper, flowing ink strokes, sparse composition with generous negative space, subtle crimson and grey palette, misty atmosphere, consistent with the earlier panels. A close view of a tomb passage on the loess plateau at night: a neat row of rough grey pottery jars and figurines at the entrance, their coarse clay textures finely rendered, catching warm moonlight. Behind them, distant mountain ridges stand like a screen under a full round moon, and a lone Han soldier holding a spear keeps watch with his back to the viewer. Cold, quiet, austere mood. Chinese calligraphic title inscription: "陶罐·陪葬". No other text.
