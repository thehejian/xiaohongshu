Ink wash painting style on light rice paper, flowing ink strokes, sparse composition with generous negative space, subtle crimson and grey palette, misty atmosphere, exaggerated dramatic mood. A crowded Western Han court hall: a throng of officials in coarse hemp robes covered in patches stand in a circle, all weeping loudly with exaggerated grief, tears streaming, some wailing with raised sleeves, one pounding the floor. At the center, an emperor in a plain coarse robe wails the loudest with head thrown back. Absurd, theatrical, over-the-top competition of tears. No gore, no violence. Chinese calligraphic title inscription: "哭穷大会". No other text.
