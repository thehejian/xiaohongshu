Ink wash painting style on light rice paper, flowing ink strokes, sparse composition with generous negative space, subtle crimson and grey palette, misty atmosphere, consistent with the first panel. An exaggerated contrast: an emperor in coarse hemp robe sits on a stone step, grimacing as he forces himself to eat a bowl of plain beans and wild greens held in a rough clay bowl, while directly behind him through an open doorway a lavish banquet table is faintly visible piled with rich dishes, a contrast of self-inflicted poverty. Ironic, theatrical, comical mood. Chinese calligraphic title inscription: "吃素·作秀". No other text.
