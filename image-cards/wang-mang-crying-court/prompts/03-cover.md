Ink wash painting style on light rice paper, flowing ink strokes, sparse composition with generous negative space, subtle crimson and grey palette, misty atmosphere, consistent with the earlier panels. An absurd courtyard scene: a Confucian scholar in plain robes practices wailing loudly in front of a bronze mirror, mouth wide open, eyes squeezed shut, rehearsing his grief performance, while on a stool beside him lies a neatly folded new official robe and hat waiting to be worn. Comical, shameless, opportunistic mood. No gore, no violence. Chinese calligraphic title inscription: "哭得好·封官". No other text.
