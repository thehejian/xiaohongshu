# Content Analysis: 为什么只有商鞅成功了

## Content Type
Historical comparison / political analysis

## Topic
Comparing three major reformers of the Warring States period — Li Kui (Wei), Wu Qi (Chu), Shang Yang (Qin) — and why only Shang Yang's reforms survived.

## Key Points
- Li Kui: Economic reform only, no political restructuring → system died with him
- Wu Qi: aggressive noble suppression, relied solely on king's support → reversed after his death
- Shang Yang: institutionalized reforms, created merit-based beneficiary class → survived his execution
- Three critical innovations: military merit system, institutional detachment from ruler, destroyed old nobility's power base

## Audience
Chinese history fans, political/institutional reform enthusiasts

## Hook Potential
High. "Why Shang Yang succeeded where others failed" is a classic historical puzzle. Three-case comparison is engaging.
