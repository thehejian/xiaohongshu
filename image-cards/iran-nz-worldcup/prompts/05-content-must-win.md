Xiaohongshu (Little Red Book) infographic in screen-print poster style, portrait 3:4.

## Core Principles
- Screen print / silkscreen poster art — flat color blocks, NO gradients
- Bold silhouettes and symbolic shapes over detailed rendering
- Negative space as active storytelling element
- One iconic focal point per image — conceptual, not literal

## Color Rules (CRITICAL)
- 2-5 FLAT COLORS MAXIMUM
- Duotone pair: Burnt Orange #E8751A + Deep Teal #0A6E6E
- Off-black background #121212
- Halftone dot patterns for tonal variation (NOT gradients)
- Slight color layer misregistration for print authenticity

## Text Style (CRITICAL)
- Bold condensed sans-serif lettering
- Typography INTEGRATED into composition as design element
- High contrast with background, stencil-cut quality
- DO NOT use delicate, thin, or handwritten fonts

## Composition
- Geometric framing: circles, arches, triangles
- Figure-ground inversion where possible
- Stencil-cut edges between color blocks, no outlines
- Paper grain texture beneath all colors

## Layout: Balanced
- Top-weighted title, evenly distributed content below
- 40-50% whitespace, 3-4 info points

## Content
- Title: 「第三重：非赢不可」
  - Iran: 小组头名是底线，首战抢分战意极强
  - NZ: 16年后重返世界杯，赢一场就是历史首胜
  - 弱队无包袱，强队满背锅
  - 背水一战的伊朗 vs 放手一搏的新西兰

**Visual**: Balance scale composition. Left side heavy with cracked stone weight (Iran's burden), right side light with feather (NZ free spirit). Off-black background. Orange for Iran side, teal for NZ side. Bold title text integrated into scale beam. Halftone dots in shadows.
