Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. A mountain completely surrounded by thousands of Xiongnu cavalry arranged in four colored groups — white horses on the west, blue on the east, black on the north, red on the south — forming a terrifying ring of horsemen, no gap to escape, overwhelming siege. Chinese title 四色合围 in calligraphic brush style. Subtitle 四面铁骑插翅难逃. No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
