Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Han iron lamellar armor (札甲) on soldiers, ring-pommel dao (环首刀), Han crossbow (弩), tense battlefield atmosphere with charging cavalry, dust and urgency. Han and Xiongnu generals exchanging sides across the battlefield. Chinese title "汉匈双面" in calligraphic brush style.
