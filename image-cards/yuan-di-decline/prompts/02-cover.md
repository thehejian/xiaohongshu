Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle grey and faded crimson colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere, melancholic mood. Han dynasty palace in autumn. A powerful eunuch Shi Xian sitting at the main desk receiving memorials while officials bow, in the distant empty hall a single figure — Emperor Yuan — walks away alone with hunched shoulders, his back expressing defeat and resignation. Falling leaves. Chinese title '"中书弄权"' in calligraphic brush style.
