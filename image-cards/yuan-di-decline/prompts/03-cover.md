Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle grey and faded crimson colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere, melancholic mood. Han dynasty desolate landscape. A young woman in red Han robe sitting in a simple horse-drawn carriage heading north, lifting the curtain to look back one last time, an empty abandoned Han beacon tower in the foreground, withered grass, grey sky, the Han flag tattered and barely hanging. The scene conveys the sadness of a once-great empire retreating. Chinese title '"昭君·秋风"' in calligraphic brush style.
