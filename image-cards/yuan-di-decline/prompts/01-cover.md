Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle grey and faded crimson colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere, melancholic mood. Han dynasty palace study. Emperor Yuan sitting alone at his desk in dim candlelight, reading Confucian classics with a pale expression, administrative scrolls piled up and collecting dust around him, his shadow long and lonely on the wall. Empty granaries visible through the window. The emperor looks lost and helpless. Chinese title '"纯任德教"' in calligraphic brush style.
