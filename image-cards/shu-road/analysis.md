# Content Analysis: 蜀道难

## Content Type
Historical geography / travel narrative

## Topic
The ancient roads into Sichuan and why they were incredibly dangerous

## Key Points
- Sichuan surrounded by mountains on all sides
- Jinniu Road + Jianmen Pass - the main route, impenetrable fortress
- Yinping Road - Deng Ai's death march
- Micang Road - narrow, no carts
- Ziwu Road - shortcut that killed everyone who tried
- Plank roads on cliffs (栈道) - rotting wood, certain death
- Sichuan as China's best natural fortress

## Audience
Chinese history fans, geography enthusiasts
