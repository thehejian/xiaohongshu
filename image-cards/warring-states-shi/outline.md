# Outline: 战国士文化

Style: screen-print, 6 images

**Layout**: sparse (cover)
**Visual**: Silhouettes of ancient Chinese scholars from different schools gathering together — one holds a book (儒家), one points at the sky (道家), one holds a legal scroll (法家), one holds a sword (兵家). Group portrait of intellectuals.
**Text**: 战国士文化
**Subtitle**: 诸子百家为何群星闪耀

**Text**: 贵族政治崩塌 知识流入民间
**Subtitle**: 以前只有贵族能学的现在人人都能学
