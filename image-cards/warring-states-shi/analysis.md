# Content Analysis: 战国士文化

## Content Type
Historical-cultural analysis

## Topic
The rise of the 士 (shi) class during the Warring States period and how it enabled the Hundred Schools of Thought

## Key Points
- Zhou aristocracy collapsed → knowledge flowed to the people
- Confucius first to teach commoners (有教无类)
- 士 class emerged as knowledge workers
- Free movement between states (no borders for talent)
- Rulers competed for talent (稷下学宫, golden platform)
-百家争鸣: Confucianism, Daoism, Legalism, Mohism, etc.
- After unification,思想大一统, never again such brilliance
