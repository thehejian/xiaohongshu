Ink wash painting style on light rice paper, flowing ink strokes, sparse composition with generous negative space, subtle crimson and grey palette, misty atmosphere, consistent with the first panel. A colossal giant of a man, towering a full head and shoulders above normal soldiers, wearing Han armor, stands before an army holding a powerful tiger on a thick leash; on a city wall behind him, an emperor in dark imperial robes with a flat-topped crown points at the giant with eager excitement. Absurd, awe-inspiring, fantastical mood. No gore, no violence. Chinese calligraphic title inscription: "巨人·驱虎". No other text.
