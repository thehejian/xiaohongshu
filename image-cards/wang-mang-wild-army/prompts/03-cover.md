Ink wash painting style on light rice paper, flowing ink strokes, sparse composition with generous negative space, subtle crimson and grey palette, misty atmosphere, consistent with the earlier panels. A battle scene rendered through banners: on one side, a small band of rebel soldiers with a modest few banners charges forward under a low siege wall; facing them, an immense sea of enemy banners stretches to the horizon, an overwhelming wall of flags. The contrast of a tiny force against a vast host. Epic, tense, dramatic mood. No gore, no bodies, no blood. Chinese calligraphic title inscription: "昆阳·以少胜多". No other text.
