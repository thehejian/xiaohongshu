Ink wash painting style on light rice paper, flowing ink strokes, sparse composition with generous negative space, subtle crimson and grey palette, misty atmosphere, dynamic exaggerated mood. A chaotic battlefield scene: startled war elephants, tigers and leopards stampede in a panic through a Han army formation, soldiers scrambling and scattering in all directions, a giant tiger banner knocked over and trampled underfoot. Dramatic, absurd, comic-tragic energy. No gore, no bodies, no blood. Chinese calligraphic title inscription: "虎豹之师·反向". No other text.
