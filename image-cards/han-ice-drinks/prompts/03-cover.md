Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Summer scene. Han dynasty noble household in the sweltering heat — ice cooling the room, crushed ice mixed with honey and fruit in a lacquer bowl as an early cold dessert, chilled wine beside it. The pleasure of ice in high summer. Chinese title '"冰饮·消暑"' in calligraphic brush style.
