Ink wash painting style on light rice paper, flowing ink strokes, sparse composition with generous negative space, subtle crimson and grey palette, misty atmosphere, consistent with the previous panels. A satirical street scene outside a grand mansion gate in Chang'an: a crowd of commoners has gathered, some feigning sorrow with raised sleeves, others craning necks and slipping glances toward the now-closed gate as if coveting valuables inside; a lone cart stands ready with bags, hinting at departure and confiscation. Tongue-in-cheek, darkly humorous mood, no violence. Chinese calligraphic title inscription: "哭第实窃". No other text.
