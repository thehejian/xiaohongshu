Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Split composition: left side a Han iron foundry with furnace, bellows, and workers forging ring-pommel swords (环首刀); right side vast grasslands with thousands of horses in imperial pastures. A Han cavalryman in lamellar armor riding in the center. Chinese title "钢铁与马政" in calligraphic brush style, subtitle "环首刀·四十万骑". No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
