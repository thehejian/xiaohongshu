Ink wash painting with subtle color tints, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, delicate pale colors peeking through, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. A Tengri (Tuyuhun) royal court on the shores of a vast inland sea: white felt tents in a line along the grassland, a khan on horseback receiving a group of envoys offering tribute, the calm lake and distant alpine mountains in soft morning mist. no text, no calligraphy, no writing.
