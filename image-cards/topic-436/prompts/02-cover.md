Ink wash painting with subtle color tints, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, delicate pale colors peeking through, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. A founding court scene at Pingcheng: a young Xianbei khan in leather armor seated on a wooden throne inside a rough-walled palace hall, Xianbei nobles in robes and Han officials in silk standing in mixed rows, a large map of the north hanging on the wall behind them. no text, no calligraphy, no writing.
