Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Han dynasty scene. A young woman in elegant Han dress sitting by a desk reading scrolls, a scholar-teacher pointing at a text, bamboo books piled around. In the background, another woman taking an examination at court. The education and literacy of Han dynasty women. Chinese title '"女子读书·不输须眉"' in calligraphic brush style.
