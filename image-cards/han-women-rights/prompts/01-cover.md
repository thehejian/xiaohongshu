Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Han dynasty scene. A woman in Han dynasty dress writing a divorce contract at a desk, her husband looking shocked and humiliated behind her, a bailiff stamping the document. Her dowry boxes stacked beside her, ready to leave. The legal right of a Han woman to divorce her husband. Chinese title '"妻休夫·卷嫁妆走人"' in calligraphic brush style.
