Ink wash painting style on light rice paper, flowing ink strokes, sparse composition with generous negative space, subtle crimson and grey palette, misty atmosphere. Strong visual contrast of age: a dignified elderly princess in her fifties, hair grey-streaked, wearing elegant cross-collared deep robes (曲裾深衣) and a high hair bun, sits in a place of honor, while beside her a handsome young man of about sixteen or seventeen, boyish and charming, leans in intimately, raising an authentic Western Han lacquer ear-cup (漆耳杯, an oval wooden cup with two ear-like handles) of wine toward her. Court servants in the background glance at each other and smirk. Whimsical, scandalous, ironic mood. No emperor, no dragon motifs. Chinese calligraphic title inscription: "黄昏恋". No other text.
