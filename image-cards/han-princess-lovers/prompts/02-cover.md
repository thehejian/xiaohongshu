Ink wash painting style on light rice paper, flowing ink strokes, sparse composition with generous negative space, subtle crimson and grey palette, misty atmosphere, consistent with the first panel. Absurd role reversal: a Western Han emperor in plain dark cross-collared robes with a flat-topped crown (玄衣通天冠, no dragon motifs) bows deeply with both hands clasped in greeting toward a young commoner man in plain robes who stands relaxed and unbothered, chin slightly raised. Behind them the elderly princess looks on with an amused, proud smile. Court attendants gape in disbelief. Ironic, comical mood. Chinese calligraphic title inscription: "皇上叫哥". No other text.
