Ink wash painting style on light rice paper, flowing ink strokes, sparse composition with generous negative space, subtle crimson and grey palette, misty atmosphere, consistent with the earlier panels. Contrast of origins: outside a Western Han princess mansion, an elegant princess in cross-collared deep robes stands proudly beside a tall handsome general in polished Han armor and helmet. Beside them, tethered to a post, is a plain old horse and a simple wooden cart — hints of his former lowly life as a stable groom. A quiet, knowing smile passes between the two. Chinese calligraphic title inscription: "骑奴·将军". No other text.
