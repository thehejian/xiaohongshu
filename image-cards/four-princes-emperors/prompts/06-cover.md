Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Four emperor portraits arranged in staggered composition. Top left: Han Wen Di Liu Heng, wearing black-and-gold Han imperial robe, kind and gentle expression, books of governance beside him. Top right: Deposed Emperor Liu He, crown askew, robes disheveled, panicked expression, scattered edicts behind him. Bottom left: Han Xuan Di Liu Xun, black-and-gold Han robe, calm and restrained demeanor, hand on sword hilt. Bottom right: Ming Jiajing Emperor Zhu Houcong, bright yellow dragon robe with five-clawed dragon pattern, cold and commanding presence. Each portrait labeled with large calligraphic name characters. Background blending faint silhouettes of Chang'an ancient walls and the Forbidden City. Large calligraphic title at top center: 四种命运. Small subtitle: 会等·急着·能忍·硬刚. Ancient Chinese people, accurate imperial robes for each dynasty. No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
