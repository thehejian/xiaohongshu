Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Outside Zuo Shun Gate of the Forbidden City, Ming dynasty. Over three hundred officials in court robes kneeling across the square, a sea of dark figures. Some weeping with faces to the sky, others prostrated on the ground. Inside the gate, the edge of Jiajing Emperor's bright yellow dragon robe faintly visible. Dark clouds gathering above. Large calligraphic characters on the right: 明世宗朱厚熜. Small subtitle: 大礼议·满朝跪谏. Ancient Chinese people, Jiajing in Ming bright yellow dragon robe, officials in red and blue Ming court robes. No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
