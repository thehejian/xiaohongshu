Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Outside Chang'an city gate, early Han dynasty. Prince of Dai Liu Heng's carriage stopped before the gate. Liu Heng lifting the curtain, gazing at Chang'an city with cautious expression. Zhou Bo and Chen Ping waiting to welcome him at the gate. Fresh snow after clear sky. Large calligraphic characters on the left: 汉文帝刘恒. Small subtitle: 先探路，再进城. Ancient Chinese people, Liu Heng in Han dynasty black-and-gold imperial robe, officials in Han court dress. No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
