Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Han dynasty abdication ceremony in the great hall. Wang Mang kneeling to receive the jade imperial seal from young Ruzi Ying (Liu Ying), the boy's tiny hands reluctantly handing it over. Empress Dowager Wang Zhengjun standing aside, her face a mix of grief and helplessness. The end of the Western Han dynasty after 200 years. Chinese title '"禅让·西汉终"' in calligraphic brush style.
