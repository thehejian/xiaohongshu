Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Han dynasty court scene. Wang Mang sitting on the dragon throne in imperial robes but with a banner reading 'Acting Emperor' beside him, a two-year-old toddler on a cushion nearby. Officials kneeling before him. The line between 'acting' and 'real' grows thinner by the day. Chinese title '"假皇帝"' in calligraphic brush style.
