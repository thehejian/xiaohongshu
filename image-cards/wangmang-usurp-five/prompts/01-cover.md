Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Han dynasty throne hall. Wang Mang accepting the Nine Bestowments (jiu xi) — receiving nine ceremonial gifts from the emperor one by one, kneeling in false humility while his eyes reveal ambition. Court officials bowing deeply around him. The peak of official power before the final push. Chinese title '"加九锡"' in calligraphic brush style.
