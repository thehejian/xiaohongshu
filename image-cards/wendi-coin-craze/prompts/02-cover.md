Ink wash painting style on light rice paper, flowing ink strokes, sparse composition with generous negative space, subtle crimson and grey palette, misty atmosphere, consistent with the first panel. A mountain copper mine in western China: a favored court official in Han robes stands on a cliff overlooking miners hauling ore from a copper mountain, molten bronze being poured into coin moulds at a furnace below, stacks of newly cast coins piled high. Wealth and privilege in a landscape setting. Chinese calligraphic title inscription: "邓氏钱". No other text.
