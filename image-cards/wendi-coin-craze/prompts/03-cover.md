Ink wash painting style on light rice paper, flowing ink strokes, sparse composition with generous negative space, subtle crimson and grey palette, misty atmosphere, consistent with the earlier panels. A poor thatched-room exterior at dusk: a ragged former court official sits by the low door, head bowed in despair, empty sleeves hanging, a single broken coin discarded on the ground beside him. A servant woman gestures sorrowfully nearby. Desolate, ironic ending mood — the man who once minted the whole empire's money now penniless. No violence. Chinese calligraphic title inscription: "一文不值". No other text.
