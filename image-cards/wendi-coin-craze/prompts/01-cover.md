Ink wash painting style on light rice paper, flowing ink strokes, sparse composition with generous negative space, subtle crimson and grey palette, misty atmosphere. A Western Han emperor in dark ceremonial robes with a flat-topped imperial crown sits on a low dais in a plain palace hall (no dragon carvings), gesturing with an open palm toward a set of scattered bronze coins and casting furnaces below — symbolically granting the people the right to mint money. Bronze-smelting furnaces and moulds sit in the foreground, steam rising. Whimsical, ironic mood. Chinese calligraphic title inscription: "与民共铸". No other text.
