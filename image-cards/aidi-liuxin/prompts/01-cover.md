Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Han dynasty palace chamber with low bed and floor mats. A young emperor in his twenties in Han court robe with crossed-collar and guan headpiece, carefully cutting his own sleeve with a sword. Beside him sleeps a beautiful young man — clearly male, delicate features, wearing Han official's robe — his head resting on the emperor's sleeve. The intimate tenderness of the famous "cut sleeve" story. Chinese title '"断袖"' in calligraphic brush style.
