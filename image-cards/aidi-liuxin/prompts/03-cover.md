Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Han dynasty palace bedchamber. A very young man in his mid-twenties lying dead on a bed, wearing Han dynasty sleeping robe, his face young and peaceful. An elderly formidable woman in formal Han empress dowager robes rushing in, reaching for the jade imperial seal. Attendants in Han court dress scattering in panic, curtains billowing. The raw power seizure at the moment of a young emperor's death. Chinese title '"尸骨未寒·玉玺易手"' in calligraphic brush style.
