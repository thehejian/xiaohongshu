Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Han dynasty court scene. A very young emperor in his early twenties sitting on the throne in formal Han court dress with crossed-collar shenyi and tall guan crown, casually gesturing as if offering the realm. Beside him stands a handsome young man in minister's robe looking surprised. Before them, elderly ministers in Han official robes recoil in shock and horror. Chinese title '"禅让玩笑"' in calligraphic brush style.
