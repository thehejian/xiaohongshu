Ink wash painting style on light rice paper, flowing ink strokes, sparse composition with generous negative space, subtle crimson and grey palette, misty atmosphere, consistent with the earlier panels. An exaggerated official registry scene: a stern Han clerk with a brush crosses out long double-character names on a family genealogy scroll, replacing them with rows of simple single-character names like "Sun One, Sun Two, Sun Three"; he shoves a new name register at a stunned extended family to press thumbprints, while an old patriarch gapes at the ruined genealogy scroll and a small child is dragged forward to be renamed. Absurd, oppressive, comical mood. No gore, no violence. Chinese calligraphic title inscription: "一律·单名". No other text.
