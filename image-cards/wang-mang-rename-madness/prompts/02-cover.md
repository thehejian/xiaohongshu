Ink wash painting style on light rice paper, flowing ink strokes, sparse composition with generous negative space, subtle crimson and grey palette, misty atmosphere, consistent with the first panel. An absurd commotion at the gate of the Han capital: soldiers haul away the old city-gate plaque while officials hoist a giant wooden board inscribed with new characters up toward the gate arch; below, puzzled townspeople mill about — an old man pats his head staring at the unfamiliar plaque, a traveler with a letter wanders in circles scratching his head, a child points and laughs. Exaggerated, comical, chaotic confusion. No gore, no violence. Chinese calligraphic title inscription: "长安·常安". No other text.
