Ink wash painting style on light rice paper, flowing ink strokes, sparse composition with generous negative space, subtle crimson and grey palette, dynamic exaggerated mood. A manic Western Han emperor in dark imperial robes with a flat-topped crown (no dragon motifs) sits amid a swirling storm of wooden nameplates, official seals, maps and bamboo scrolls that fly around him like blizzard snow. He grips a map in one hand and a nameplate in the other, eyes wide with fanatical excitement, ink brush in his mouth, cross-out marks and new names written on everything. Chaotic, absurd, obsessive, comically over-the-top energy. Chinese calligraphic title inscription: "改名·狂魔". No other text.
