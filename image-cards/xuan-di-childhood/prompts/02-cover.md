Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Han dynasty commoner hemian robe with simple cloth belt, no guan, loose hair or simple cloth headwrap. A teenage boy in patched commoner clothes watching cockfights on a dusty Chang'an street, with market stalls and ordinary people in the background, clay jars and bamboo baskets. The boy's posture is lively and unrefined. Chinese title '"长安街头的野孩子"' in calligraphic brush style.
