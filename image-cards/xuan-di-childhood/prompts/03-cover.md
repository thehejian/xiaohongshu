Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Han dynasty shenyi robe with crossed-collar right closure, traditional topknot with simple guan. A young man in patched robes stands on the street looking toward the distant Weiyang Palace rooftops visible through mist, a commoner village girl holding his hand. Humble thatched houses and a vegetable garden nearby. The contrast between the imperial palace in the distance and the ragged youth is striking. Chinese title '"市井少年，未央在望"' in calligraphic brush style.
