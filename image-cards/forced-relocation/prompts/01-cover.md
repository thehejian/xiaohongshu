Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. A grand procession of ox-carts and horse-drawn wagons loaded with household goods, wealthy Han dynasty families in silk robes reluctantly leaving their estates, soldiers escorting them. Dusty road leading toward a distant city wall. Chinese title "徙陵收割" in calligraphic brush style, subtitle "三百万·悉迁茂陵". No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
