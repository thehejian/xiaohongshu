Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. A Han princess in ornate Chinese silk robes standing among Wusun nomads on the Central Asian steppe, felt tents (yurts) in the background, her face melancholy and homesick. Contrast between elegant Chinese attire and the rugged nomadic setting. Chinese title "细君和亲" in calligraphic brush style, subtitle "宗室女·远嫁乌孙". No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
