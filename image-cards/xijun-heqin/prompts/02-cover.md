Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. A grand Han procession crossing the Gobi Desert, hundreds of officials and attendants, imperial carriages loaded with silk and gold, camel trains stretching to the horizon. Dust rising from the long march. Chinese title "天价嫁妆" in calligraphic brush style, subtitle "乘舆服御·浩浩荡荡". No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
