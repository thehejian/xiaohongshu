Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. A Han princess in silk robes sitting alone inside a felt yurt, playing a pipa (Chinese lute), tears on her face. Outside the tent opening, endless grassland. A single yellow bird (huanghu) flying toward the distant horizon. Chinese title "悲愁歌" in calligraphic brush style, subtitle "愿为黄鹄兮归故乡". No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
