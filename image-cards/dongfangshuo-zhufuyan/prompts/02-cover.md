Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Dongfang Shuo, nine feet tall, entering the Han palace with his self-recommendation letter of three thousand bamboo slips carried by two men. Emperor Wu reading the lengthy scrolls. A legendary first appearance. Ancient Chinese people in Han clothing. Chinese title "东方" in calligraphic brush style. Subtitle "三千简".
