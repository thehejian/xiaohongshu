Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Zhu Fuyan presenting the Enfeoffment Reduction Decree (推恩令) to Emperor Wu — princely domains being divided among all sons, shrinking generation after generation, the central power growing stronger without direct action. Ancient Chinese people in Han court clothing. Chinese title "推恩" in calligraphic brush style. Subtitle "不削自弱".
