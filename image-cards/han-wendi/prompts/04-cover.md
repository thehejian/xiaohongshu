Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. A dramatic scene in heavy rain before the grand Han palace gate. The young girl Ti Ying, no more than fifteen, kneeling on the wet stone steps in soaked plain clothes, both hands raising a bamboo slip document high above her head toward the closed palace gate. Rain streaming down her face mixing with tears. The palace gate is tall and imposing with cold brass studs, firmly closed yet slightly ajar as if someone inside has heard. Huge scale contrast between the tiny figure and the massive gate. Character labeled "少女缇萦" in foreground. Chinese title: 血泪上书. Subtitle: 一个小女孩敲响帝国的大门.
