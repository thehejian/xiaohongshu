Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Outside Chang'an city gate of early Han dynasty. A Han prince's carriage has stopped on the road. Prince Liu Heng (future Han Wen Di) standing beside the carriage, wearing simple dark prince robe, his expression caught between tension and relief. His uncle Bo Zhao has just galloped back on horseback, dismounting and reporting with clasped hands that the city is safe. A few attendants behind, no soldiers. The moment of decision before entering the unknown capital. Characters labeled "代王刘恒" and "薄昭". Chinese title: 渭桥探路. Subtitle: 舅舅先行，安全再进. Ancient Chinese people, early Han prince and messenger clothing. No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
