Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. A rural village scene of early Han dynasty. A village head (lizheng) standing on a stone platform beating a gong with one hand, holding an imperial edict scroll in the other, loudly announcing "Tax exemption this year!" The farmers in the field have frozen mid-motion — one has dropped his hoe, another is kneeling with hands clasped in gratitude, expressions shifting from shock to joy. Sunlight breaking through clouds above. No single character label, the focus is on the collective moment of realization. Chinese title: 免税天下. Subtitle: 十二年不收粮. Ancient Chinese people, Han dynasty peasants and village official clothing. No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
