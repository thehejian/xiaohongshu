Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Tender final bedside scene of Western Han dynasty. Emperor Wen Di Liu Heng lying on a simple sickbed in plain undyed hemp sleeping robe, face gaunt but eyes peaceful — NO gold, NO dragon patterns. Crown Prince Liu Qi (future Han Jing Di) kneeling beside the bed, holding his father's hand tightly, head bowed, expression grieving yet respectful. Prince Liu Qi wearing a simple dark blue Western Han prince robe, NO golden/yellow robe, NO dragon patterns. Wen Di's lips slightly parted as if whispering final words — "love the people." Outside the window, the first light of dawn breaking over Chang'an city. Only a single candle on a plain wooden table, no luxury. Characters labeled "汉文帝刘恒" (bed) and "太子刘启" (kneeling). Chinese title: 临终托付. Subtitle: 爱民. Ancient Chinese people, early Western Han dynasty, emperor in plain hemp sleeping robe, prince in dark blue Han court robe. No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
