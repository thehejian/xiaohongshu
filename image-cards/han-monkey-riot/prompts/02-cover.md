Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, vivid crimson and grey colors, dramatic composition, misty atmosphere. A chaotic, almost comical scene of mass escape: dozens of lean, wild-eyed monkeys explode through a shattered wooden cage door in a surging wave of fur and fury, scrambling over each other, leaping onto trees, swinging from broken fences. A startled zookeeper in shabby robes sprawls backward on the ground, his empty grain bucket rolling away, a few scattered kernels of grain the only evidence of the meager rations that sparked the rebellion. Broken bamboo, twisted metal, splintered wood, the imperial zoo in ruins, a bizarre prison break of imperial animals. Chinese title "群猴越狱" in large bold calligraphic brush style.
