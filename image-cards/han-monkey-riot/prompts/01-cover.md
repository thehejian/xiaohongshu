Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, vivid crimson and grey colors, dramatic composition, misty atmosphere. An absurd, grotesque scene at a Han dynasty imperial mausoleum: a troop of wild-eyed monkeys with mangy fur has completely taken over the sacred tomb. One bold monkey sits triumphantly on the emperor's stone spirit tablet, clutching a half-eaten ritual apple, chattering mockingly. Another monkey dangles from a ceremonial banner, ripping the silk. A third monkey squats on a stone stele, urinating down at a broken official cap lying in the dust. Overturned bronze vessels, scattered offerings, torn silk curtains, the ultimate desecration of imperial dignity by lowly beasts. Darkly comic, bizarre, and deeply unsettling. Chinese title "猴占皇陵" in large bold calligraphic brush style.
