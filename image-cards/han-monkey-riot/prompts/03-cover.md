Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, vivid crimson and grey colors, dramatic composition, misty atmosphere. A bizarre, darkly comic scene at the foot of a monkey-infested mausoleum: a group of high-ranking Han officials in splendid court robes stand helplessly looking up, their dignified composure shattered. One minister shakes his fist in impotent rage as a monkey on a tree branch mockingly imitates his gesture. Another official shields his head with a scroll as a half-eaten fruit flies toward him. A third official sits on the ground, having slipped on a scattered offering, his official cap askew. The great Han empire reduced to a farce by a troop of clever monkeys, absurd humiliation, misty twilight. Chinese title "束手无策" in large bold calligraphic brush style.
