# Content Analysis: 苏秦的逆袭

## Content Type
Rags-to-riches biography / inspirational story

## Topic
Su Qin (苏秦) — from a poor nobody mocked by his own family to the prime minister of six states simultaneously

## Key Points
- Started as a poor farmer's son in Luoyang
- Studied under Guiguzi, failed at persuading Qin
- Family scorned him → drove him to study harder (悬梁刺股)
- Changed strategy: vertical alliance (合纵) against Qin
- Convinced all six states, wore six prime minister seals
- Returned home in glory
- Assassinated in 284 BC, died still scheming

## Audience
Motivational/inspirational seekers, Chinese history fans

## Recommended Plan
- Strategy A (Story-Driven): Fall → Study → Rise → Glory
- Style: screen-print
- 6 images
