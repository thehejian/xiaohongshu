Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. The scene splits into two contrasting halves — top half: elderly Zhou Bo in battered armor, white-haired and rough-faced, stands before the North Army camp gate shouting with one arm raised high, his expression bold and unrefined like a reckless old warrior. Bottom half: young Zhou Yafu in pristine armor stands at the Xiliu garrison gate, posture straight, face expressionless, giving a formal military salute to Emperor Wen, disciplined and reserved. Their difference in age, bearing, and character is stark — one crude and passionate, the other precise and guarded. The silhouette of Weiyang Palace fades in distant mist.
