Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Inside the dark Tingwei prison, Zhou Yafu sits alone on a straw mat, hair disheveled, face gaunt and emaciated, a bowl of rice untouched before him. A sliver of sky light penetrates through the iron window, illuminating his stubborn, despairing face. A jailer's shadow lurks in the corner. The composition is oppressive and bleak with ample whitespace. Ancient Chinese man, Han dynasty prison clothing. Chinese title "条侯饿死" in calligraphic brush style. No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
