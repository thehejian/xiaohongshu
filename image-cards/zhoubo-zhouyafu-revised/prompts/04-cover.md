Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. A stark contrast of two elderly statesmen in the Weiyang Palace court. On the left, elderly Zhou Bo stands trembling with shame, white-haired and weathered face, sweat dripping from his forehead, hands clasped nervously, head bowed — a once-mighty general reduced to panic. On the right, elderly Chen Ping stands calmly with a composed face, also white-haired but elegant, hands clasped serenely, speaking smoothly. Both are old men, but one is panicked and the other composed. Emperor Wen sits on the dragon throne between them, eyes fixed coldly on Zhou Bo. Court officials whisper behind their sleeves. Ancient Chinese elderly people, Han dynasty court clothing. Chinese title "汗流浃背" in calligraphic brush style. No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
