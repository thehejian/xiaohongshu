Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. The city of Suiyang is ablaze with fire and smoke, King Liu Wu of Liang in armor desperately commands the defense from the walls, while Wu-Chu allied troops surge like a tide beneath. In the distance at Changyi, Zhou Yafu's army remains entrenched in deep ditches and high walls, motionless. The two areas are separated by a wide river. The composition splits in two — left side blazing with battle, right side silent stillness. Ancient Chinese people, Han dynasty military clothing. Chinese title "以梁委之" in calligraphic brush style. No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
