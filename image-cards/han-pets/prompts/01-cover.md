Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. A dramatic scene at the Han dynasty imperial court: a kennel official (gou jian) in dark robes kneels presenting a magnificent hunting hound with a gleaming gold collar to the emperor on a raised dais, while in the shadow of a pillar a young scholar in plain robes peeks out, watching with yearning — the poor scholar about to be recommended to the emperor by this very dog keeper. Tension and hidden ambition in the air, lamplight, ancient court splendor. Chinese title "靠狗上位" in calligraphic brush style.
