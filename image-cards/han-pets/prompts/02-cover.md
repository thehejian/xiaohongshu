Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Inside the palace of a Han dynasty prince, a worried palace attendant in court robes kneels holding up a wooden tablet (mudu) as he reports the lost dog to the prince, who leans forward frowning, curious and attentive. On the table, the official wooden tallies recording the palace dogs lie stacked. A carved wooden dog statue stands in a corner. The slightly absurd official drama of a lost palace dog, warm lamplight, solemn yet humorous mood. Chinese title "寻狗·奏报" in calligraphic brush style.
