Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. At a cold Han dynasty frontier pass under a crescent moon, border soldiers in armor inspect a herd of tall war horses by torchlight, one soldier measuring a horse's shoulder height with a measuring rod while another records details on a bamboo scroll register by lantern light. Frost on the ground, mist curling, the horses standing in solemn rows with breath steaming. The strict military management of strategic horses. Chinese title "战马·管制" in calligraphic brush style.
