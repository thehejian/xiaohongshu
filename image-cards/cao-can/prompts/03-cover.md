Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Sickbed scene of Xiao He. Xiao He leaning against the bed on the left side of the composition, face gaunt but expression clear and composed, one hand weakly pointing toward the right to recommend. On the right side of the composition, Cao Can standing beside the bed, wearing chancellor court robe, bowing respectfully with hands clasped, listening attentively. Dim candlelight in the room, medicinal aroma in the air. Characters labeled "萧何" (left, in bed) and "曹参" (right, standing). Xiao He in sleeping robe, Cao Can in Han chancellor court robe. Chinese title: 萧何荐参. Subtitle: 临死只说了两个字. Ancient Chinese people, Han dynasty clothing. No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
