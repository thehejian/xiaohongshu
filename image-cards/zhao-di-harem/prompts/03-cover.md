Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. A Han dynasty bedchamber scene: very young Emperor Zhao around 21 on his deathbed, his face pale and young, an empty cradle beside the bed symbolizing no heir. The empress Shangguan at 15 kneels nearby in white mourning robes, still a girl herself. Scattered qunku garments lie discarded on the floor — the absurd tool of control rendered meaningless. Chinese title '“绝嗣·竹篮”' in calligraphic brush style. No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
