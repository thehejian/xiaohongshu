Ink wash painting style on light rice paper, flowing ink strokes, sparse composition with generous negative space, subtle crimson and grey palette, misty atmosphere. Interior of the grand Western Han Qilin Pavilion in Weiyang Palace: on the walls hang eleven long vertical portrait scrolls of officials — several in formal dark court robes with tall hats, others in leather armor — each with a small inscription above. In the center stands a Han emperor in plain dark cross-collared robes with a flat-topped crown (玄衣通天冠, no dragon motifs), gazing up at the portraits with reverence. Solemn, monumental, auspicious mood. Chinese calligraphic title inscription: "麒麟阁". No other text.
