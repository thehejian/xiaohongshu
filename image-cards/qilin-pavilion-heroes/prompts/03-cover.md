Ink wash painting style on light rice paper, flowing ink strokes, sparse composition with generous negative space, subtle crimson and grey palette, cold northern atmosphere, consistent with the earlier panels. A snowy wasteland in the far north: a gaunt, grey-bearded elderly Han envoy in worn fur-trimmed robes stands in deep snow, clutching a tall ceremonial jie staff (汉节) whose tassels are nearly all frayed and gone, surrounded by a small flock of sheep. Behind him a vast frozen lake and blizzard. His expression is resolute and unyielding. Solemn, heroic, enduring mood. Chinese calligraphic title inscription: "持节·十九年". No other text.
