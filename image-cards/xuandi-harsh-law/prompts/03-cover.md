Ink wash painting style on light rice paper, flowing ink strokes, sparse composition with generous negative space, subtle crimson and grey palette, misty atmosphere, consistent with the earlier panels. A city courtyard at dusk south of the Han capital: a deep open pit in the earth, a tall stone marker stele standing beside it, uniformed officers escorting a group of bound young street toughs toward the pit, a low city wall and watchtower silhouetted behind. Solemn, severe, ironic mood. No explicit violence. Chinese calligraphic title inscription: "虎穴". No other text.
