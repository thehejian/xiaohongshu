Ink wash painting style on light rice paper, flowing ink strokes, sparse composition with generous negative space, subtle crimson and grey palette, misty atmosphere, consistent with the earlier panels. Authentic Western Han material culture (202 BC - 8 AD): the southwest corner of the Han capital Chang'an at dusk, a rammed-earth city wall with a corner watchtower in the background. A Han county magistrate in dark cross-collared long robe with collar closed right over left (交领右衽) and a black official headdress (进贤冠) stands beside a deep pit in the earth. Two or three Han guards wear short coarse jackets (短褐) with cloth head wraps (帻) and hold ring-pommel swords, escorting bound young street toughs in cross-collared coarse robes toward the pit. A simple wooden signpost beside the pit bears the characters "虎穴". Solemn, severe, ironic mood. No explicit violence. Chinese calligraphic title inscription: "虎穴". No other text.
