Ink wash painting style on light rice paper, flowing ink strokes, sparse composition with generous negative space, subtle crimson and grey palette, misty atmosphere, consistent with the first panel. The interior of a Western Han county office hall: a stern-faced magistrate with a long beard presides over a desk, rows of kneeling prisoners in coarse clothes line up before him, a stack of wooden verdict tablets at hand. An elderly mother in plain traveling robes stands at the doorway, raising her hand in horrified reproach. Tense, dramatic mood. Chinese calligraphic title inscription: "屠伯". No other text.
