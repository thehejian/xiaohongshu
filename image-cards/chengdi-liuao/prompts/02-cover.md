Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Han dynasty palace scene. Zhao Hede pointing angrily at a baby wrapped in cloth, Emperor Cheng sitting on the floor covering his face and crying helplessly, unable to protect his own son. A eunuch looking down in shame. The dark tragedy of imperial succession. Chinese title '"燕啄皇孙"' in calligraphic brush style.
