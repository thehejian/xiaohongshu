Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Han dynasty palace hall with low tables and mats. Zhao Feiyan dancing on a bronze tray held by a servant, her robes flowing, Emperor Cheng sitting slack-jawed and mesmerized in dim lamplight, administrative scrolls piled and dusty beside him. The empire decays while he watches. Chinese title '"掌上飞燕"' in calligraphic brush style.
