Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Outside the Northern Army camp. Lv Lu on horseback about to leave, several attendants behind him. Hesitant expression on his face, holding a troop-command tally in his hand. A scholar-official messenger standing beside him, smiling with hands cupped in polite salute. Zhou Bo's carriage approaching the camp in the distance. Ancient Chinese people, Han dynasty prince and scholar-official clothing. Chinese title 巧夺兵权 in calligraphic brush style. Subtitle 陈平一计，吕禄上当. No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
