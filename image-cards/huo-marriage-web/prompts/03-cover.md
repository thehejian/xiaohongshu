Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Han dynasty grand mansion with rammed-earth walls and tiled roof. Luxury horses and carriages in the courtyard being confiscated by imperial officials, silk and gold being loaded onto carts, a fallen family signboard. The composition suggests wealth turning to ash. Chinese title '"权倾·覆灭"' in calligraphic brush style.
