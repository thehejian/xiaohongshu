Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Lady Gouyi kneeling before Emperor Wu, removing her hair ornaments to beg for mercy. The Emperor coldly commands: "Quickly go, you shall not live!" The brutal moment of "kill the mother, establish the son." Ancient Chinese people in Han court clothing. Chinese title "趣行" in calligraphic brush style. Subtitle "不得活".
