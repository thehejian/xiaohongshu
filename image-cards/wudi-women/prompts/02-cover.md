Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Young Liu Che as a child telling his cousin Chen Ajiao: "If I marry Ajiao, I will keep her in a golden house." A fairy-tale beginning to a political alliance. Ancient Chinese children in Han period clothing. Chinese title "金屋" in calligraphic brush style. Subtitle "童话".
