Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Three women of Emperor Wu's life interwoven: Chen Ajiao leaning against a golden chamber, Wei Zifu holding scrolls with gentle humility, Lady Gouyi with jade hook standing in shadow. Three different fates beneath one emperor. Ancient Chinese women in Han court attire. Chinese title "三个女人" in calligraphic brush style.
