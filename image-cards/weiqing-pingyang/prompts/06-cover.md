Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space. At dusk, two tombs face each other across the Maoling mausoleum grounds — the large one shaped like Mount Lu (Wei Qing's tomb), and the smaller one to the east (Princess Pingyang's sheep-head tomb). The setting sun casts warm, desolate light, creating a scene of eternal serenity. Ancient Chinese scene. Chinese title "同葬茂陵" in calligraphic brush style. Subtitle "从骑奴到驸马，此生不负". No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
