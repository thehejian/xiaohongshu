Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space. Princess Pingyang rides on horseback through the Chang'an countryside. Behind her, young Wei Qing — a horse groom — leads the horse as an attendant. Spring grass spreads green around them. The figures of mistress and servant reflect each other under the vast sky. Ancient Chinese people, Han dynasty noble and common clothing. Chinese title "骑奴卫青" in calligraphic brush style. Subtitle "平阳公主的三嫁". No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
