Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, vivid crimson and grey colors, dramatic composition, misty atmosphere. A massive, chaotic scene of mass hysteria: thousands of people in authentic Western Han clothing — men in short coarse brown hemp robes (短褐) with hair in topknots, women in long curved-lapel robes (曲裾深衣) with crossed collars, all barefoot and disheveled, eyes wild with fear — surge across a dusty countryside road in a great wave. Some clutch wooden tokens to their chests, others wave burning torches, some beat drums and gongs frantically. Dust rises from their running feet, stretching as far as the eye can see. A few have stumbled by the roadside, still reaching out with their tokens. The incredible spectacle of an entire population swept up in religious frenzy, misty landscape, dramatic scale. Chinese title "西王母狂潮" in large bold calligraphic brush style.
