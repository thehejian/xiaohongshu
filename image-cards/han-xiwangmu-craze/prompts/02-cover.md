Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, vivid crimson and grey colors, dramatic composition, misty atmosphere. A dynamic scene of chain-reaction mania: villagers in authentic Western Han clothing run frantically from one village to another — men in short brown hemp robes (短褐) with crossed collars and cloth head wraps, women in long curved-lapel robes (曲裾深衣) with flowing sashes — passing wooden tokens from hand to hand like a relay race. A farmer in a straw hat and coarse hemp tunic has abandoned his plow and ox and joined the run, token in hand. A woman in a plain undyed curved-robe passes a token through a wooden window to another woman. On a hill in the background, a long line of people with torches snakes across the landscape like a fiery dragon. The speed and madness of mass hysteria spreading through the countryside, dazed faces, urgent motion. Chinese title "传筹狂奔" in large bold calligraphic brush style.
