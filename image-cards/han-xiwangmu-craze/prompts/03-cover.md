Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, vivid crimson and grey colors, dramatic composition, misty atmosphere. A surreal scene of mass hysteria reaching the capital: a massive crowd of people in authentic Western Han clothing surges like a tide before the towering gates of the Han Weiyang Palace — the majestic East Que Gate with twin watchtowers (双阙) on each side, massive rammed-earth (夯土) walls in grey-brown, dark wooden beams and gray tiled roofs, a large vermillion wooden door in the center. Men in short brown hemp robes (短褐) and dark crossed-collar tunics, women in long hemp-curved robes (曲裾深衣), some dancing wildly with sleeves raised, others kneeling and praying with wooden tokens raised high, many climbing trees and the rammed-earth walls to get a better view, shouting and singing. Palace guards in black lacquered armor and helmets stand helplessly at the vermillion gate, overwhelmed by the sheer number of people. Torches flicker everywhere in the night, creating a sea of fire. The capital city of an empire consumed by collective madness, misty night scene. Chinese title "京城狂热" in large bold calligraphic brush style.
