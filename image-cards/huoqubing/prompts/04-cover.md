Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Climax of the Mobei campaign: Huo Qubing ascends Mount Langjuxu (present-day Khentii Mountains, Mongolia) to build an altar and perform the Heaven sacrifice ceremony. Below lies the vast North Sea (Lake Baikal). Han banners rise over the northern frontier for the first time. Soldiers stand solemnly behind him, wind whipping the banners. Huo Qubing in general armor with sword at the altar summit. Soldiers in Han armor standing at attention. Chinese title "封狼居胥" in calligraphic brush style. No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
