Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. The Hexi Campaign: Huo Qubing leads ten thousand cavalry across Yanzhi Mountain and Qilian Mountain, six days marching a thousand li. Snow-capped mountains in the distance, Han cavalry riding along the mountain base. On the right side, King Hunxie surrenders with over forty thousand tribesmen in a long winding column. Huo Qubing in Han armor on horseback atop the mountain. Han cavalry in light armor. Xiongnu surrendering in tribal clothing. Chinese title "河西之战·打通走廊" in calligraphic brush style. No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
