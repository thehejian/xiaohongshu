Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Zhang Qian's legacy: a camel caravan crossing the newly opened Silk Road, carrying Western Regions goods — Dayuan blood-sweating horses, Daxia bamboo staffs, Parthian treasures. The beginning of the Silk Road stretching across the desert. Ancient Chinese and Central Asian traders. Chinese title "丝路" in calligraphic brush style. Subtitle "开端".
