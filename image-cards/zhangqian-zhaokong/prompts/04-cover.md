Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. The Great Yuezhi court in their new homeland of Daxia, people living peacefully, unwilling to go to war against the Xiongnu. Zhang Qian's diplomatic plea falling on deaf ears, a scene of failed negotiation. Ancient Chinese envoy and Central Asian people in period clothing. Chinese title "月氏" in calligraphic brush style. Subtitle "安不愿战".
