Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Zhang Qian travelling westward after escaping from the Xiongnu, passing through Dayuan (Fergana) and Kangju toward the Great Yuezhi — snow-capped mountains, vast grasslands, and distant trade routes of the Western Regions. Ancient Chinese traveller in Han clothing. Chinese title "西行" in calligraphic brush style. Subtitle "大宛·月氏".
