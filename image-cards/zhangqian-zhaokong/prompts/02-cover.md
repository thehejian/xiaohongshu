Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Zhang Qian held captive by the Xiongnu for ten years, inside a Xiongnu tent, gripping his Han staff with determination, his Xiongnu wife and child nearby. A scene of quiet endurance and loyalty. Ancient Chinese people in Han and Xiongnu clothing. Chinese title "被俘" in calligraphic brush style. Subtitle "持节".
