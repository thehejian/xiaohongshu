Ink wash painting style on light rice paper, flowing ink strokes, sparse composition with generous negative space, subtle crimson and grey palette, misty atmosphere, consistent with the first panel. A Western Han palace study: three opened wooden document chests sit on a low desk, their scrolls spilling out. A young official in plain cross-collared robes writes rapidly from memory at the desk, brush moving, while a Han emperor in plain dark robes with flat-topped crown looks on with surprise and admiration, leaning forward. Impressive, marveling mood. Chinese calligraphic title inscription: "过目不忘". No other text.
