Ink wash painting style on light rice paper, flowing ink strokes, sparse composition with generous negative space, subtle crimson and grey palette, misty atmosphere. A divided composition showing father and son of the Western Han: on the left, a stern, imposing official in formal dark court robes with tall hat, sharp piercing eyes, holding a verdict tablet — the harsh prosecutor. On the right, a gentle, composed scholar in plain cross-collared robes, calm expression, holding a scroll. The two figures face slightly away from each other, suggesting two very different eras and temperaments. Ironic, contrasting mood. No gore. Chinese calligraphic title inscription: "父酷吏·子贤臣". No other text.
