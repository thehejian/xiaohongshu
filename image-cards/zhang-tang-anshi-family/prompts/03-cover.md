Ink wash painting style on light rice paper, flowing ink strokes, sparse composition with generous negative space, subtle crimson and grey palette, misty atmosphere, consistent with the earlier panels. A Western Han study at dusk: two brothers face each other across a low table — the elder one, warmly dressed, gestures eagerly proposing a marriage, while the younger one in plain robes raises a hand in firm refusal, shaking his head with a grave, cautious expression. A red marriage proposal card lies on the table between them. Tense, prudent, guarded mood. Chinese calligraphic title inscription: "不争·不显". No other text.
