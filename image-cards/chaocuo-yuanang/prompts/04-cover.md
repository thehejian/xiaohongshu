Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Inside Chao Cuo's residence, he discusses matters with an advisor, war reports from the seven kingdoms spread on the table. The advisor reaches out a hand to stop him, his face anxious. Outside the window in the night, a servant hurries away as if to deliver a message. Ancient Chinese people, Han dynasty clothing. Chinese title "走漏风声" in calligraphic brush style. Subtitle "一念之差，生死逆转". No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
