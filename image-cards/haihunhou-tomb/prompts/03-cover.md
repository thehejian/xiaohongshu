Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Han dynasty period setting with appropriate clothing and architecture. A stark contrast: in the foreground, piles of gold ingots and horse-hoof gold; in the background, a lone crippled figure (Liu He in his final years) slumped in worn-out clothes, half-paralyzed, staring into emptiness. The gold glitters but brings no comfort — a gilded cage. Chinese title '"金山囚徒"' in calligraphic brush style, subtitle '"黄金·牢笼"'. No heavy outlines.
