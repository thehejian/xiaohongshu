Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, vivid crimson and gold colors, dramatic composition, misty atmosphere. A lavish scene inside a Han dynasty palace hall: a young emperor in black silk robes sits on a cushioned low couch, gazing with captivated admiration at a slender dancer in flowing crimson silk sleeves who twirls gracefully on a circular crystal tray held aloft by a strong palace attendant. Silks and lanterns drape from the ceiling, bronze lamps flicker, jade ornaments gleam. The intoxicating luxury of imperial indulgence, the legendary "palm dance" of Zhao Feiyan, opulence and romance. Chinese title "掌上舞" in large bold calligraphic brush style.
