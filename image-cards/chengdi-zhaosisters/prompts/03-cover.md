Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, vivid crimson and gold colors, dramatic composition, misty atmosphere. An intimate scene inside a Han dynasty palace chamber: a beautiful woman in flowing silk robes delicately holds a small golden pill box, one pill resting in her palm — a fragrant dark orb emitting a faint wisp of aromatic smoke. Behind her, a carved wooden screen, a bronze mirror, silk curtains. The legendary "Xiji Wan" (flesh-firming pill) that gave a captivating fragrance but carried a hidden cost. Bittersweet beauty, ancient mystery, rich warm colors. Chinese title "息肌丸" in large bold calligraphic brush style.
