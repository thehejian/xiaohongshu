Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, vivid crimson and gold colors, dramatic composition, misty atmosphere. A magnificent view inside the legendary Zhaoyang Palace of the Han dynasty: white jade steps lead up to a grand hall, walls inlaid with blue jade (lantian bi) that shimmer with a cool green glow, gold ornaments accenting every pillar and beam, luminous pearls scattered like stars along the ceiling. A lone beautiful woman in luxurious silk robes stands by a window, gazing out at the misty garden, her reflection in the polished jade floor. The ultimate palace luxury built for a beloved consort, extravagant beauty, dreamlike atmosphere. Chinese title "昭阳宫" in large bold calligraphic brush style.
