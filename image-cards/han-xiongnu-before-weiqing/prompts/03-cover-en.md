Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Before a Han dynasty palace gate, the emperor's four-horse carriage has mismatched coat colors—some white, some brown—showing the scarcity. Noble officials ride ox-drawn carts. In sharp contrast on the right side: the Xiongnu steppe with thousands of galloping horses and riders on horseback from childhood. The crippling lack of warhorses in early Han. Ancient Chinese people, Han emperor in ceremonial robes, officials in court attire, Xiongnu herders in fur. Chinese title "无马之朝" in calligraphic brush style. No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
