Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Mount Baideng in a harsh winter snowstorm. Emperor Liu Bang's Han army is surrounded for seven days, soldiers' fingers frozen and falling off. Below the mountain, layers of Xiongnu cavalry form an impenetrable ring, their colored horse formations creating a wall of steeds. Han forces cannot get in or out. Ancient Chinese people, Liu Bang in royal robes on the mountain, Han soldiers in winter armor, Xiongnu riders in fur on horseback. Chinese title "白登之围·七日" in calligraphic brush style. No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
