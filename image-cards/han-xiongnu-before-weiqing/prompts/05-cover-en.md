Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. The Wen and Jing emperors' horse-breeding program. Thirty-six imperial horse ranches across border commanderies, with 300,000 horses tended by government slaves. Horses gallop between fields, fill the streets and lanes—a scene of quiet preparation. The Han dynasty patiently building its military strength for the counterattack to come. Ancient Chinese people, Han horse herders in short hemp clothing, officials in robes inspecting. Chinese title "文景马政·养马三十万" in calligraphic brush style. No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
