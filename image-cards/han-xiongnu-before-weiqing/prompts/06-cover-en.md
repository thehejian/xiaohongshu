Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. A long horizontal scroll composition. Envoy Lou Jing holds imperial tally leading a tribute caravan northward to seal the peace treaty—silks, wine, grain for the Xiongnu. The seventy-year road of patience stretches across the scroll. At the far right end of the painting: dawn light breaks, Emperor Wu raises his arm, and Wei Qing's iron cavalry ride out of the pass—the end of submission and the beginning of counterattack. Ancient Chinese people, Lou Jing in Han envoy robe, tribute carriers in Han clothing, distant Emperor Wu in ceremonial robes and Wei Qing in general armor emerging from the light. Chinese title "和亲七十年·终见曙光" in calligraphic brush style. No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
