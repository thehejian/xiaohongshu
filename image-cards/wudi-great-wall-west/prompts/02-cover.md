Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. A vast desert landscape with a line of beacon towers disappearing into the distance, from the oasis city of Dunhuang (with its crescent moon lake) toward the salt flats of Lop Nur. Camels and a Han cavalry patrol along the route. Chinese title "敦煌·罗布泊" in calligraphic brush style, subtitle "烽燧线·两千里". No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
