Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, vivid crimson and grey colors, dramatic composition, misty atmosphere. A chaotic, absurdly overcrowded street scene in ancient Chang'an: a flood of women in fine palace robes spills out of the massive palace gates, clutching bundles and jewelry boxes, their faces a mix of tears and laughter. But the street beyond is already packed — grinning bachelors, shifty-eyed merchants, rough-looking men, all pushing and shoving, grabbing at sleeves, waving coins. One woman is being pulled in two directions, another fends off a grabby hand with her bundle. A vegetable cart is overturned, a stray dog runs through the chaos. The grand imperial release turned into a city-wide farce, darkly comic pandemonium. Chinese title "三千出宫" in large bold calligraphic brush style.
