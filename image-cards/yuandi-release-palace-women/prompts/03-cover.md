Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, vivid crimson and grey colours, dramatic composition, misty atmosphere. A scene of official helplessness: a weary Han city magistrate (Jing Zhaoyin) stands in the middle of a chaotic street, his official cap askew, sweat dripping from his brow, both hands raised in a gesture of surrender as chaos swirls around him. Armored soldiers try half-heartedly to form a line, but the crowd of women, merchants, and opportunists surges past them. A soldier shrugs at another. A woman negotiates with a jeweler right in front of a patrol. The absurd spectacle of imperial authority completely powerless, the empire's decline laid bare on the streets, darkly comic defeat. Chinese title "京兆尹投降" in large bold calligraphic brush style.
