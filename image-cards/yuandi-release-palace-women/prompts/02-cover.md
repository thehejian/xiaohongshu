Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, vivid crimson and grey colours, dramatic composition, misty atmosphere. A ridiculous, over-the-top street brawl in Han Chang'an: a crowd of desperate men shoves and fights over a young woman in palace robes who looks utterly bewildered, clutching her bundle to her chest. Two men pull her arms in opposite directions, a third tries to grab her sleeve, while a fourth argues with a merchant over a jade bracelet she dropped. A granny with a broom whacks a pickpocket. A donkey cart loaded with goods is stuck in the chaos. Dust, flying silk, shouting, pushing — the imperial capital reduced to a chaotic free-for-all marriage market, wild absurdity and dark humor. Chinese title "抢亲大乱" in large bold calligraphic brush style.
