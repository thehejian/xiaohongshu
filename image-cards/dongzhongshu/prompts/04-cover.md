Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Dong Zhongshu presenting his famous argument to Emperor Wu in the Han court: "Let the hundred schools be silenced, let only the Six Arts and Confucius' teachings advance." Confucian scrolls being elevated while other philosophical texts are set aside. Ancient Chinese people in Han court clothing. Chinese title "罢黜" in calligraphic brush style. Subtitle "独尊儒术".
