Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. A divided scene: on one side Emperor Wu's court with Confucian scholars performing elegant rituals (outer face of benevolence); on the other side prison chambers with harsh instruments of Legalist punishment — the cruel official Zhang Tang presiding. "Confucian crown, Legalist sword." Ancient Chinese figures in Han period. Chinese title "阳儒" in calligraphic brush style. Subtitle "阴法·霸王道".
