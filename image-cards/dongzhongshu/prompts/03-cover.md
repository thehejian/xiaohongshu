Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. The concept of Heaven-Man Induction: an emperor looking up at the sky with reverence as auspicious signs and natural disasters are sent as heavenly messages. The connection between celestial and earthly realms. Ancient Chinese emperor in Han court robes. Chinese title "天人" in calligraphic brush style. Subtitle "感应".
