Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. A tall multi-story Han dynasty tower (飞廉观) rising through mist and clouds, with a large bronze mythical bird (飞廉) perched on the topmost roof. The bird has a deer body and bird head, its wings spread as if about to take flight. Above the tower, the moon and stars are visible. The emperor stands on the top floor looking out. Mystical and elevated atmosphere. Chinese title "飞廉观·铜风鸟" in calligraphic brush style, subtitle "居高住观·待天音". No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
