Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. A Han dynasty palace interior at night: an emperor in robes sitting at a low desk covered with bamboo slips, writing by candlelight with a carving knife on unusually long bamboo strips (三尺). The strips are stacked beside him, noticeably longer than ordinary ones. One slip lies on the floor showing its full length. Chinese title "三尺简·亲写" in calligraphic brush style, subtitle "五十四年·亲手不辍". No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
