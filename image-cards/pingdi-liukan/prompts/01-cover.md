Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Han dynasty throne hall. A tiny nine-year-old boy in oversized Han imperial robe sitting on the massive dragon throne, his feet not touching the floor, looking lost and frightened. Behind the throne looms the tall silhouette of Wang Mang in minister's robes, one hand resting on the throne's back, controlling everything. The puppet and the puppeteer. Chinese title '"九岁傀儡"' in calligraphic brush style.
