Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Han dynasty throne hall. Wang Mang sitting on the dragon throne wearing imperial robes but still labeled as "acting emperor," a two-year-old toddler in baby clothes sitting beside him on a cushion, crying. Court officials bowing below. The final step before full usurpation of a 200-year dynasty. Chinese title '"摄皇帝"' in calligraphic brush style.
