Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Han dynasty palace bedchamber. Wang Mang kneeling beside a sick young boy on a bed, holding out a bowl of dark medicine with a gentle smile that does not reach his eyes. The boy emperor looking at the bowl with uncertainty. An ominous atmosphere of impending death. Chinese title '"毒药·慈颜"' in calligraphic brush style.
