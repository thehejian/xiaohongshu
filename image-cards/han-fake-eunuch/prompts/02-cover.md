Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. A dramatic scene on the road to the Han imperial hunt: a magnificent ornate carriage (fucha) trimmed with gold, banners flying, followed by a hundred armored riders in gleaming armor, sweeping past in a cloud of dust. A prince in royal purple robes kneels low in the roadside dust, head bowed in a deep respectful salute, mistaking the carriage for the emperor's own. Inside the carriage a handsome young man in black silk robes glances out with a faint smile of arrogance as the wheels roll past the prostrating prince. The cruel humiliation of a favorite outranking a royal prince, dramatic tension, swirling dust and mist. Chinese title "误拜副车" in calligraphic brush style.
