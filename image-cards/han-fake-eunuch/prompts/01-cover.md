Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. A dramatic Han dynasty moment at the imperial hunting grounds: a young emperor in dragon robes and a handsome young man in black silk robes sit close together on a stone bench before their horses, sharing a single open scroll, the young man leaning in eagerly to point at the text — an intimate bond that breaks all court rules, other guards and courtiers watching from a distance with shocked, whispered expressions. The shock of a man who is not a eunuch yet shares the emperor's bed and heart, tension and scandal in the air, lamplight and mist. Chinese title "常与上卧起" in calligraphic brush style.
