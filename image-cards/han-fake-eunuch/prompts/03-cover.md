Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. A tragic dramatic scene inside the grand hall of the Han dowager empress: the stern empress dowager in deep crimson robes sits high on her throne, face like stone, one hand resting on a silk decree sealed with vermilion. Before her kneels the young emperor in dragon robes, hands clasped in desperate supplication, while beside him a young man in black robes stands pale and resigned with head bowed, awaiting his fate. An imperial messenger in formal robes stands by the door holding the death warrant. The cruel collision of imperial favor and iron palace rules, tragedy and dread, flickering candlelight against ancient pillars, swirling shadows. Chinese title "赐死" in calligraphic brush style.
