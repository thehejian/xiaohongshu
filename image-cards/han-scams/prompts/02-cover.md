Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, vivid crimson and grey colors, dramatic composition, misty atmosphere. A dramatic courtroom scene in a Han dynasty palace hall: a fierce-faced judge in dark red official robes slams his fist on a wooden desk, bamboo slip documents scattering, pointing an accusing finger at two shackled noblemen kneeling in disgrace before him — their crowns and ceremonial robes stripped, in plain prisoner garb, heads bowed in shame. One prisoner clutches a single plucked chicken, absurdly, as evidence of his crime. Behind the judge, a massive wall of stacked bamboo law books and an official seal, towering imperial guards with halberds on both sides. The spectacle of a marquis losing his title over a stolen chicken, over-the-top severity and dramatic justice, candlelight flickering. Chinese title "夺爵·严惩" in large bold calligraphic brush style.
