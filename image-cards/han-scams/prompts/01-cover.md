Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, vivid crimson and grey colors, dramatic composition, misty atmosphere. A chaotic, dramatic scene on a bustling Han dynasty Chang'an street: a slick-looking con man with a sly squint flings himself theatrically under the hooves of a wealthy merchant's ornately decorated horse carriage, wailing and clutching his ankle in exaggerated agony, tongue sticking out in fake pain. The carriage horse rears wildly with flaring nostrils, the driver yanks the reins in shock, while a huge crowd of market onlookers gasps, points, and grins — some laughing, some shaking heads. Around them, crowded wooden market stalls with hanging banners and goods, smoke from food stalls. An over-the-top, almost comical spectacle of ancient staged roadside fraud, high tension, vivid humor. Chinese title "碰瓷·恐猲" in large bold calligraphic brush style.
