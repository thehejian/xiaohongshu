Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, vivid crimson and grey colors, dramatic composition, misty atmosphere. An epic, heart-wrenching scene on the long road to Chang'an: a tiny teenage girl in torn plain linen robes, hair disheveled, chases desperately behind a creaking wooden prisoner cart, one hand raised clutching a scroll letter high above her head, tears streaming down her face, gritting her teeth in fierce determination. In the cart, a shackled middle-aged physician in ragged prison clothes twists around, reaching one bound hand toward her, his weathered face full of grief and tenderness. Dust swirls and wind howls around them, the vast empty road stretches to distant city walls, a huge crimson sun sinking on the horizon. An epic scale of filial courage against overwhelming despair, powerful emotional impact. Chinese title "缇萦救父" in large bold calligraphic brush style.
