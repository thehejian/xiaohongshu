Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Sima Qian enduring the humiliation of castration, bent over his desk writing the Records of the Grand Historian. His face shows pain but unyielding determination. Bamboo slips and brushes scattered around him. Ancient Chinese scholar in Han clothing. Chinese title "腐刑" in calligraphic brush style. Subtitle "著史".
