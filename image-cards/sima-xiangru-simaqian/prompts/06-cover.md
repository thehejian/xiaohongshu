Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. The scrolls of the Records of the Grand Historian transforming into a river of stars across the sky — "the unmatched masterpiece of historians, the unrhymed Lisao." A grand concluding vision. Chinese title "绝唱" in calligraphic brush style. Subtitle "离骚".
