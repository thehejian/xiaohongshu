Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Two scenes side by side: Sima Xiangru writing a grand rhapsody with flourishing brushstrokes, and Sima Qian carving bamboo slips for the Records of the Grand Historian with blood and tears. One writes glory, one writes truth. Ancient Chinese scholars in Han clothing. Chinese title "史笔与赋华" in calligraphic brush style.
