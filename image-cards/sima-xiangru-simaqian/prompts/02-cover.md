Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Sima Xiangru presenting the Zixu Rhapsody to Emperor Wu — the Emperor slapping the table in astonishment, exclaiming he wished he could have lived in the same era as the author. Ancient Chinese people in Han court clothing. Chinese title "子虚" in calligraphic brush style. Subtitle "拍案".
