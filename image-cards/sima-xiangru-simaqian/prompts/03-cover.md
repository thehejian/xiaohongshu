Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Sima Xiangru playing the zither at a banquet, Zhuo Wenjun listening from behind a screen, their hearts connecting through music — the legendary romance that led to their elopement. Ancient Chinese people in Han clothing. Chinese title "琴挑" in calligraphic brush style. Subtitle "当垆".
