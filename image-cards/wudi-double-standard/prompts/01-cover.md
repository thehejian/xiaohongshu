Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. A Han dynasty palace scene split in two: on one side, a humble shepherd (卜式) offering bags of gold to officials who turn away suspiciously; on the other side, a poet on his deathbed (司马相如) writing, while the emperor eagerly reads his scroll with delight. Chinese title "武帝双标" in calligraphic brush style, subtitle "捐家疑·献赋喜". No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
