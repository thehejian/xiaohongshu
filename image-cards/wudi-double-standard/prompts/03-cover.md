Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. A Han dynasty scene of a frail poet (司马相如) lying on a sickbed, using his last strength to write on a scroll. An attendant holds a lamp nearby. In the background, the emperor is shown reading the same scroll with a face of pure joy and excitement. Chinese title "相如·封禅书" in calligraphic brush style, subtitle "临终献赋·武帝大喜". No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
