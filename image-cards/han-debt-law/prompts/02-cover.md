Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Han dynasty fields, a debtor laboring under a creditor's watch, using a tally stick to count work days, the landowner in the distance tallying. The Han practice of 'labor repayment' — working off debt with physical labor. Chinese title '"劳役偿债"' in calligraphic brush style.
