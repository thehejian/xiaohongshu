# Outline: 巨鹿之战项羽一战封神

## 01-cover-xiangyu
**Visual**: Xiang Yu standing tall on a battlefield, holding his spear, with burning ships and broken cauldrons behind him. Smoke and fire. A young 24-year-old god of war.
**Text**: 巨鹿之战项羽一战封神
**Subtitle**: 五万楚军打垮二十万秦军

## 02-siege
**Visual**: Qin armies surrounding Julu city. Zhang Han and Wang Li's forces camped outside. Allied armies camped nearby, all afraid to fight. Zhao ruler trapped inside the city.
**Text**: 秦军围城诸侯不敢救
**Subtitle**: 二十万精锐谁敢上

## 03-cauldron-breaking
**Visual**: The iconic moment: Chu soldiers smashing cooking pots and burning ships at the riverbank. Xiang Yu giving orders. No retreat, only victory or death. Three days rations.
**Text**: 破釜沉舟背水一战
**Subtitle**: 凿沉船只砸烂锅灶只带三天干粮

## 04-nine-battles
**Visual**: Epic battle scene. Chu soldiers fighting with desperate courage. Qin formation breaking. Nine clashes over the battlefield. Xiang Yu leading from the front.
**Text**: 九战九胜秦军溃败
**Subtitle**: 楚军个个以一当十喊杀震天

## 05-allied-kneel
**Visual**: Allied generals kneeling before Xiang Yu. They enter his tent on their knees, heads bowed. Xiang Yu sitting in command. The awe of the watching armies.
**Text**: 诸侯将领膝行而前
**Subtitle**: 头都不敢抬太可怕了

## 06-ascend
**Visual**: Xiang Yu at 24, standing at the peak, all forces behind him. The road to Xianyang ahead. The turning point of history.
**Text**: 二十四岁改写中国历史
**Subtitle**: 从此一路杀向咸阳
