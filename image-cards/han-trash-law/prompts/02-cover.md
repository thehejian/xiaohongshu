Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. A misty dawn over Han dynasty Chang'an, streets empty and vast. A row of Western Han sanitation workers in cross-collar deep robes (jiaoling shenyi) with their hair tied up in simple buns, sweeping the street with bamboo brooms in unison, wet blue stone slabs revealed behind their brooms. In the distance the palace towers of Weiyang emerge faintly in the morning fog. An old man in a Han robe with gray hair gathered in a topknot gathers leaves into a pile with a bamboo basket. Chinese title '"环卫·长安晨扫"' in calligraphic brush style.
