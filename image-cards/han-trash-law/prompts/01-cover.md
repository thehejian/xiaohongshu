Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Early morning market street of Han dynasty Chang'an. A disheveled man in rough clothes bends over sweeping the street with a bamboo broom, dust swirling in the morning light. Behind him stands an official with arms crossed, hands on hips. Passersby cover their noses and point at him, children giggle and whisper. A wooden sign around his neck reads 'penalty: sweep the street for three days'. Embarrassment written across his face. Chinese title '"乱倒垃圾·罚扫街"' in calligraphic brush style.
