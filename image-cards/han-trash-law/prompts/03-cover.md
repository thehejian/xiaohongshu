Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Underground trench in Han dynasty Chang'an. Western Han workmen in cross-collar deep robes (jiaoling shenyi) with hair in topknots, crouching to lay ceramic drainage pipes — two men hoisting a round pipe to align its joint, another sealing the seam with clay. Several layers of pentagonal pipes line the trench walls, water flowing through the openings. A Western Han supervisor in an official cap (guan) holding a bamboo scroll gestures, firelight illuminating the workers' faces. Chinese title '"陶管·地下管网"' in calligraphic brush style.
