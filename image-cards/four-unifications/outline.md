# Outline: 书同文车同轨行同伦度量衡

## 01-cover-four-pillars
**Visual**: Four pillars standing side by side, each engraved with one of the four reforms. Qin dynasty symbols. Together they form a solid foundation holding up the empire.
**Text**: 书同文车同轨行同伦度量衡
**Subtitle**: 四件事把天下捏成了一团

## 02-writing-unification
**Visual**: A scribe's desk showing different scripts from six states—all different. Then a single unified Qin seal script emerging. Southerners and northerners reading the same characters.
**Text**: 书同文统一文字
**Subtitle**: 两千多年大一统的底色

## 03-weights-measures
**Visual**: Various Warring States measuring tools—different sized buckets, rulers, scales. All being replaced by standardized Qin weights and measures. Commerce simplified.
**Text**: 统一度量衡
**Subtitle**: 跨国买卖从此不用换算

## 04-social-norms
**Visual**: Different regional customs and social behaviors being replaced by unified Qin ethical standards. Laws enforcing morality. The least known but most profound reform.
**Text**: 行同伦统一价值观
**Subtitle**: 什么是对什么是错秦国说了算

## 05-empire-framework
**Visual**: The four pillars visualized as holding up the Qin empire. Script = culture, tracks = transport, measures = economy, norms = values. An interconnected system.
**Text**: 四件事合在一起才是真正的统一
**Subtitle**: 比修长城意义大得多

## 06-legacy-outlast
**Visual**: Qin dynasty fading into history, but the four pillars standing strong through subsequent Han, Tang, Song dynasties. They became the skeleton of Chinese civilization.
**Text**: 秦朝二世而亡这四样留了下来
**Subtitle**: 成了中国文明的骨架
