Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. A Han dynasty palace scene split in two: on one side, a young man on horseback holding a bow, youthful and favored; on the other side, a musician playing a string instrument, with sheet music floating upward and fading into mist. Between them, a shadowy figure of fate looms. Chinese title "武帝·宠极而戮" in calligraphic brush style, subtitle "宠之极·祸之至". No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
