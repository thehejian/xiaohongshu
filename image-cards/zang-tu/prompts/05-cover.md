Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. In a humble farmyard, young woman Zang Er holds two infant daughters in her arms, her gaze fixed on distant palace towers. Her eyes burn with defiance, ambition, and the unyielding fire of fallen nobility. Beside her, her husband Jin splits firewood, oblivious to his wife's thoughts. Ancient Chinese people, Han dynasty common clothing. Chinese title "流落民间" in calligraphic brush style. Subtitle "王族的血，不甘埋没在泥土里". No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
