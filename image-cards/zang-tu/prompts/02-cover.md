Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Inside a military tent during the Chu-Han contention, Xiang Yu personally bestows the royal seal of Yan upon Zang Tu. Zang Tu kneels receiving the seal, his expression complex — pride mixed with unease. Cold wind howls outside the tent, hinting at the instability of the realm. Ancient Chinese men, Qin-Han period military and royal clothing. Chinese title "燕王受封" in calligraphic brush style. Subtitle "项羽封的王爷，刘邦要他的命". No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
