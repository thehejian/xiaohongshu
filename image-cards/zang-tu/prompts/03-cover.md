Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. At the Yan border fortress, Zang Tu stands atop the city wall gazing southward, his armor dust-covered. Below, Yan troops muster in formation, spears and swords like a forest. A flicker of hesitation crosses his eyes, soon replaced by ambition — Liu Bang has just founded the dynasty; he gambles that Liu Bang won't dare attack. Ancient Chinese man, Han dynasty military clothing. Chinese title "裂土反汉" in calligraphic brush style. Subtitle "第一个跳出来造反的异姓王". No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
