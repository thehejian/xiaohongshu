Ink wash painting style on light rice paper, flowing ink strokes, sparse composition with generous negative space, subtle crimson and grey palette, misty atmosphere. Inside a grand Western Han palace hall (plain Han columns, no dragon carvings), a newly enthroned emperor in dark ceremonial robes with flat-topped imperial crown (mianguan, Western Han style) sits at a low lacquer desk with a long scroll of a family register spread before him, a clerk bending over to record names. At the hall steps, several commoners in plain coarse Han robes hold up their household documents, their faces a mix of bewilderment and delight. Satirical, tongue-in-cheek mood about ordinary people suddenly becoming imperial kin. Chinese calligraphic title inscription: "五姓宗室". No other text.
