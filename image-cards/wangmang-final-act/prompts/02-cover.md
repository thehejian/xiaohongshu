Ink wash painting style on light rice paper, flowing ink strokes, sparse composition with generous negative space, subtle crimson and grey palette, misty atmosphere, consistent with the first panel. Before the Hall of Light (Mingtang), a white-bearded scholar-official bows deeply as he receives an imperial edict scroll from an attendant, while behind him stretches a long line of newly ennobled descendants — including some plain-looking farmers in coarse robes being respectfully welcomed into the procession. Absurd, theatrical atmosphere of mass fake-nobility. Chinese calligraphic title inscription: "圣裔受封". No other text.
