Ink wash painting style on light rice paper, flowing ink strokes, sparse composition with generous negative space, subtle crimson and grey palette, misty atmosphere, consistent with the earlier panels. A desolate market street in Chang'an: assorted bronze coins and spade-shaped currency lie scattered on the ground, ignored by no one; a worried merchant stands behind an empty grain stall with lowered head, his shop front deserted. In the far background, palace roofs remain hazy in the dusk. Gloomy but non-violent mood. Chinese calligraphic title inscription: "宝货崩盘". No other text.
