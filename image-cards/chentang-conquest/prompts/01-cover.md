Ink wash painting style on light rice paper, flowing ink strokes, sparse composition with generous negative space, subtle crimson and grey palette, misty desert atmosphere. A determined Western Han military commander in leather armor stands on a dune with a hand on his sword, gazing toward a distant fortress city on the horizon; behind him a column of Han soldiers and cavalry with banners advances across the vast Gobi desert under a dramatic sky. Stirring, resolute, epic mood. No explicit violence, no gore. Chinese calligraphic title inscription: "虽远必诛". No other text.
