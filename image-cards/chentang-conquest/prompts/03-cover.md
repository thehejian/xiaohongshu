Ink wash painting style on light rice paper, flowing ink strokes, sparse composition with generous negative space, subtle crimson and grey palette, misty atmosphere, consistent with the earlier panels. Dawn over a smoke-wreathed Central Asian fortress city after a siege: Han banners planted on the battlement, gates thrown open, soldiers with spears standing in ranks, a lone rider on horseback racing away across the plain toward the east carrying a sealed message. Triumphant yet restrained, solemn mood. No gore, no bodies. Chinese calligraphic title inscription: "斩单于". No other text.
