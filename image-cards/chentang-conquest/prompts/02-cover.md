Ink wash painting style on light rice paper, flowing ink strokes, sparse composition with generous negative space, subtle crimson and grey palette, misty atmosphere, consistent with the first panel. A Western Han frontier command office at night, lit by a single oil lamp: a resolute officer in plain robes unrolls a forged imperial edict scroll across a desk, brushes and a carved seal beside it, while a senior commander in armor looks on anxiously with raised hand. Behind them, beyond the open door, soldiers begin to muster in torchlight. Tense, clandestine, dramatic mood. Chinese calligraphic title inscription: "矫诏·发兵". No other text.
