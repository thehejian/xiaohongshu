Ink wash painting style on light rice paper, flowing ink strokes, sparse composition with generous negative space, subtle crimson and grey palette, misty night atmosphere. Under a full pale moon, a large ancient Han burial mound looms on the horizon; several dark silhouette figures huddle around a freshly dug dark opening at its base, one holding a lantern, another lowering a rope. Scattered pottery shards lie near the opening. Eerie, tense, furtive mood. No gore, no bodies. Chinese calligraphic title inscription: "掘冢·狂潮". No other text.
