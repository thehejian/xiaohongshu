Ink wash painting style on light rice paper, flowing ink strokes, sparse composition with generous negative space, subtle crimson and grey palette, misty atmosphere, consistent with the earlier panels. An imposing Western Han imperial mausoleum with grand stone gate before it, the gate ajar, dark opening beyond. Before the gate, scattered remnants of plundered grave goods lie on the ground: a jade suit shard, a bronze vessel, scattered pottery. A lone figure walking away in the distance. Desolate, pillaged, mournful mood. No gore, no bodies. Chinese calligraphic title inscription: "茂陵·空陵". No other text.
