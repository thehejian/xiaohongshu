Ink wash painting style on light rice paper, flowing ink strokes, sparse composition with generous negative space, subtle crimson and grey palette, misty atmosphere, consistent with the first panel. A rain-soaked field after a storm: a small band of grave-robbers in coarse robes crouch among tall grass, one kneeling to examine the soil color, another pointing at a low earthen mound, a worn shovel and coiled rope on the ground. A narrow dark shaft descends into the earth behind them. Crafty, organized, predatory mood. No gore, no bodies. Chinese calligraphic title inscription: "十墓九空". No other text.
