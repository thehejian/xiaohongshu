Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, vivid crimson and grey colours, dramatic composition, misty atmosphere. An over-the-top, absurd scene of mistaken identity: a group of disheveled young drunken men in rumpled clothes stands frozen outside a grand mansion gate, their young faces shocked and scared as a crowd of armed household guards and night patrol soldiers surrounds them with spears, swords, and blazing torches. The guards' faces show fierce determination, ready to kill the "bandits." One young drunk man — the young emperor, barely in his twenties — looks particularly terrified, mouth open, hands raised in surrender. The sheer absurdity of the young Son of Heaven about to be chopped up by his own people, dramatic torchlight, chaos, dark comedy. Chinese title "刀下留人" in large bold calligraphic brush style.
