Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, vivid crimson and grey colours, dramatic composition, misty atmosphere. A hilarious reversal scene outside a grand Han mansion: the armed guards and soldiers kneel on the ground, heads bowed low, their weapons laid aside in the dirt. A young "drunken bandit" leader — the young emperor, barely past twenty — stands in the center, holding up a jade imperial token with a wry young smile, while his young drunk companions behind him burst into surprised laughter, slapping each other on the back. One guard's face is pale with shock, another hides his face in embarrassment. The household steward prostrates himself flat on the ground. The awkward comedy of a young emperor recognized during a late-night drunken adventure, flickering torchlight. Chinese title "亮牌认主" in large bold calligraphic brush style.
