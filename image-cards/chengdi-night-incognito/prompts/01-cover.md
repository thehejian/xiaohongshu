Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, vivid crimson and grey colours, dramatic composition, misty atmosphere. A ridiculous, clandestine night scene: the young Emperor of the Han dynasty, barely past twenty, dressed in shabby commoner's rags, clambers clumsily over the palace wall like a clumsy teenage thief, his silk robe catching on the bricks, one leg dangling awkwardly. His young friend Zhang Fang in slightly nicer commoner clothes grins up at him from below, ready to catch him. A palace guard stands nearby, looking the other way with a pouch of gold in his hand, pretending not to see. The sheer absurdity of the most powerful young man in the empire sneaking out of his own home to go bar-hopping, moonlight, shadowy figures, dark comedy. Chinese title "翻墙偷溜" in large bold calligraphic brush style.
