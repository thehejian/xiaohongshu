# Outline: 九鼎传说

Style: screen-print, 6 images

## 01-cover-tripods
**Layout**: sparse (cover)
**Visual**: Nine magnificent ancient bronze tripods (ding) arranged in a grand hall, three rows of three. Each is large, with intricate animal patterns and inscriptions. Warm torchlight flickering on the bronze surfaces. Mysterious and awe-inspiring.
**Text**: 九鼎传说
**Subtitle**: 大禹铸鼎 九鼎为什么是权力的象征

## 02-content-yu-cast
**Layout**: balanced
**Visual**: Yu the Great, ancient Chinese ruler in primitive royal robes, overseeing the casting of the nine bronze tripods. Workers pouring molten bronze into molds. Nine provinces represented. Ancient Chinese East Asian men.
**Text**: 大禹治水后铸九鼎定九州
**Subtitle**: 九口鼎各刻一州山川物产

## 03-content-dynasty
**Layout**: flow
**Visual**: The nine tripods being passed through dynasties: Xia court, then Shang court, then Zhou court. Each dynasty receiving the tripods in a grand ceremony. Scroll-like timeline showing transmission.
**Text**: 夏商周三千年九鼎是合法政权硬通货
**Subtitle**: 谁拥有九鼎谁就是天下之主

## 04-content-asking
**Layout**: balanced
**Visual**: King Zhuang of Chu in elaborate royal robes sitting in his military camp, looking toward the Zhou capital. He gestures toward the distant palace. An envoy from Zhou stands before him. The famous question: how heavy are the tripods?
**Text**: 楚庄王问鼎大小轻重
**Subtitle**: 在德不在鼎

## 05-content-lost
**Layout**: action
**Visual**: Three scenes: 1) The nine tripods being loaded onto carts as Qin conquers Zhou. 2) A tripod sinking into the Sishui River, ropes breaking. 3) Qin Shi Huang commanding workers at the riverbank, trying to dredge the tripod with winches.
**Text**: 九鼎沉入泗水秦始皇打捞未果
**Subtitle**: 从此九鼎成为传说

## 06-ending-legacy
**Layout**: sparse (ending)
**Visual**: Modern Chinese calligraphy of the character "鼎" in bold brush strokes, ancient and powerful. Behind it the faint ghostly outlines of the nine tripods fading into history.
**Text**: 一言九鼎问鼎中原鼎力相助
**Subtitle**: 鼎就是刻在中国语言里的权力
