# Content Analysis: 九鼎传说

## Content Type
Legendary artifact story

## Key Points
- Yu the Great cast 9 tripods from 9 provinces bronze
- Tripods engraved with mountains, products, beasts of each province
- Symbol of legitimate rule: Xia → Shang → Zhou
- King Zhuang of Chu asked about weight (问鼎中原)
- Lost after Qin conquered Zhou
- Qin Shi Huang tried to retrieve from river but failed
- Word "鼎" still means power in Chinese today
