Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Han dynasty black-and-red lacquerware, flowing cloud patterns (yunqi wen) and mythical beasts painted on the surface, vibrant vermilion on deep black, each brushstroke dynamic and alive. The unrestrained vitality of Han craftsmanship. Chinese title '"漆器·云气纹"' in calligraphic brush style.
