Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Han dynasty official script (li shu) carved on a stone stele, bold brushstrokes with distinctive 'bo zhe' wave-like flourishes, characters bold and powerful, each stroke pushing forward. The spirit of an era captured in stone. Chinese title '"汉隶风骨"' in calligraphic brush style.
