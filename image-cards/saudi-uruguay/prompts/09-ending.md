# Card 9 — Ending Statement

Create a Xiaohongshu infographic series ending card following these guidelines:

## Image Specifications
- **Type**: Infographic Ending Card
- **Orientation**: Portrait
- **Aspect Ratio**: 1:1 (1024x1024)
- **Style**: Screen print / silkscreen poster art

## Core Principles
- Screen print / silkscreen poster art — flat color blocks, NO gradients
- Bold silhouettes and symbolic shapes over detailed rendering
- Negative space as active storytelling element
- One iconic focal point per image — conceptual, not literal
- Cream background #FAF7F2, deep text #1E293B

## Text Style (CRITICAL)
- Bold condensed sans-serif or Art Deco influenced lettering
- Typography INTEGRATED into composition as design element
- High contrast with background, stencil-cut quality
- **DO NOT use delicate, thin, or handwritten fonts**
- **SMALLER text size** to prevent overlap
- **Keep ample whitespace** around text blocks

## Color Rules
- **2-5 FLAT COLORS MAXIMUM** — fewer colors = stronger impact
- Halftone dot patterns for tonal variation (NOT gradients)
- Paper grain texture beneath all colors

- Title: 亚洲足球的崛起

Green and white scarves in the stands behind them forming a sea of color. Maximum whitespace above the scene. Bold flat shapes. Halftone textures. Cream background #FAF7F2, deep text #1E293B. Emotional triumphant ending.
