Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. A Han dynasty palace scene split in two: on one side, a witty scholar (东方朔) laughing and gesturing while the emperor smiles; on the other side, an empress (卫子夫) in full regalia fading into shadow, a general's armor on the ground. The contrast of imperial favor rising and falling. Chinese title "武帝宠爱学" in calligraphic brush style, subtitle "东方分寸·卫氏兴衰". No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
