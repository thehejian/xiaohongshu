Ink wash painting style on light rice paper, flowing ink strokes, sparse composition with generous negative space, faint crimson and grey palette, misty hazy atmosphere, matching the first panel. Inside a grand Western Han palace hall (regular deep-red lacquered pillars, NOT dragon-carved golden thrones), assembled officials bow low in an audience. Prominent foreground figure is a crisp-robed adviser pointing boldly toward the sky and denouncing the phenomenon with conviction; the seated emperor on his low dais looks grave and concerned, while beside him a richly dressed courtier (a powerful brother-in-law of the empress dowager) bows his head in discomfit.length of behalf of courtiers fully bowed low dust settles faint yellow over the hall threshold. Chinese calligraphic title inscription: "朝堂示警". No text other than the title.
