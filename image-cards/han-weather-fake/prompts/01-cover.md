Ink wash painting style on light rice paper, flowing ink strokes, sparse composition with generous negative space, faint crimson and grey palette, misty hazy atmosphere. A vast yellow dust storm sweeps in from the northwest over the Western Han capital Chang'an, blanketing the sky with ochre haze. In the foreground, characteristic Han-dynasty palace eaves and watchtower silhouette faintly emerge through the swirling yellowish dust. At the palace steps stands a lone emperor in dark black-hemmed ceremonial robes with a tall "tongtianguan" headdress (Western Han style, NOT dragon-robe patterns), gazing up solemnly at the descending airborne dust. Chinese calligraphic title inscription: "黄雾四塞". No text other than the title.
