Ink wash painting style on light rice paper, flowing ink strokes, sparse composition with generous negative space, faint crimson and grey palette, misty hazy atmosphere, matching the earlier panels. A restrained, somber scene: a middle-aged scholar wearing plain Han dynasty dark deep-robe (深衣) with hair tied up topknot, head cloth, is being led away by two attendants in dark uniforms, stepping under the palace corridor; he turns his head back toward a low desk laden with scrolls and case files, his expression resigned and heavy. Through a window, the same dull yellowish haze lingers outside. No blood, no killing, no corpses. Chinese calligraphic title inscription: "直臣京房". No text other than the title.
