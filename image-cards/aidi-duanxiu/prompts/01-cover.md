Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, vivid crimson and grey colors, dramatic composition, misty atmosphere. A quiet, intimate scene inside a Han dynasty palace chamber: a young emperor in black silk robes sits up on a wide sleeping mat, holding a drawn sword in one hand, a freshly cut section of sleeve lying beside him. Beside him, a handsome young man in white sleeping robes continues to sleep soundly, unaware, his head resting on the other end of the severed sleeve. Silk curtains drape around the bed, a bronze lamp flickers dimly, moonlight streams through a latticed window. The tender and famous origin of the "cut sleeve" idiom, quiet affection and poetic devotion. Chinese title "断袖" in large bold calligraphic brush style.
