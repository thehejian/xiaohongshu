Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, vivid crimson and grey colors, dramatic composition, misty atmosphere. A dramatic scene of rise and fall: on one side of a split composition, a lavish mansion with towering gates, gold ornaments, and rows of carriages and attendants — the peak of a young favorite's power. On the other side, the same mansion now stands silent and dark, imperial guards in armor carrying away chests of gold and silk, sealing the gates with official strips. Piles of confiscated treasures, bamboo accounting slips listing vast sums. The swift fall from supreme favor to utter ruin, a cautionary tale of fleeting imperial grace. Chinese title "四十三万万" in large bold calligraphic brush style.
