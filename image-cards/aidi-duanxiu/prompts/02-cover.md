Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, vivid crimson and grey colors, dramatic composition, misty atmosphere. A dramatic scene inside the grand hall of the Han imperial court during a banquet: the young emperor sits on a low throne, a golden wine cup in hand, his face flushed with drink, gazing at a handsome young man in court robes beside him as if to say something extraordinary. The handsome young man looks surprised and flustered. A court official in dark robes kneels urgently before the throne, arms raised in desperate protest, face full of shock. Bronze lamps, silk banners, the tension of an emperor's shocking joke about giving away the throne. Chinese title "禅让戏言" in large bold calligraphic brush style.
