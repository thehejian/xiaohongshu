Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Inside the Changle Palace, Empress Dowager Wang Zhi embraces her long-lost daughter Jin Su tightly after more than twenty years apart, both weeping with joy. Emperor Wu stands nearby, watching with a gentle smile. Sunlight streams through the grand palace windows. Ancient Chinese people, Han dynasty palace clothing. Chinese title "母女重逢" in calligraphic brush style. Subtitle "二十年，终于又见到了". No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
