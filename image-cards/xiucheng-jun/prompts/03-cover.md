Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. In the Han palace, favored minister Han Yan whispers into young Emperor Wu's ear. The Emperor's eyes widen in shock and delight — he has an elder sister living among the common people. Official documents on his desk are pushed aside as he immediately prepares to leave the palace. Ancient Chinese men, Han dynasty court clothing. Chinese title "惊天秘密" in calligraphic brush style. Subtitle "为何不早说！". No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
