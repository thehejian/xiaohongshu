Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. In the Changling farmhouse courtyard, armed guards burst through the gate. Jin Su panics and hides under the bed. Emperor Wu strides in, sees this, bends down and reaches out to help her up, sighing: "Elder sister, why hide so deep!" Jin Su is both frightened and moved, tears streaming down her face. Sunlight pours through the doorway, casting the siblings' long shadows. Ancient Chinese people, Han dynasty court and common clothing. Chinese title "姐弟相认" in calligraphic brush style. Subtitle "大姊，何藏之深也". No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
