Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. A memory scene: in the Huaili countryside, Zang Er pulls her daughter Wang Zhi away hurriedly, leaving young Jin Su behind. Little Jin Su cries and chases after them a few steps before falling to the ground. Her father Jin Wangsun rushes over to pick her up. In the distance, the carriage carrying her mother fades away — they will not see each other for twenty years. Ancient Chinese people, Han dynasty common clothing. Chinese title "骨肉分离" in calligraphic brush style. Subtitle "母亲被带走，去做皇后了". No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
