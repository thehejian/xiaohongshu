# Content Analysis: 楚国的尴尬

## Content Type
Historical analysis / why-explainer

## Key Points
- Chu was largest state by territory (11 modern provinces)
- But always lost in Warring States
- Reasons:
  1. Big but underdeveloped, sparse population
  2. Feudal system, noble infighting
  3. Loose cultural integration of conquered tribes
- Case study: Bai Qi easily pierced through Chu
- Zhao smaller but fought harder
