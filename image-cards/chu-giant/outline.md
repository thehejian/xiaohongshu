# Outline: 楚国的尴尬

Style: screen-print, 6 images

## 01-cover-chu-map
**Layout**: sparse (cover)
**Visual**: A huge map of Chu territory covering 11 modern provinces, towering over other Warring States. But the map looks hollow and vulnerable, with cracks spreading from the edges.
**Text**: 楚国的尴尬
**Subtitle**: 明明最大为什么总挨打

## 02-content-big-vs-strong
**Layout**: comparison
**Visual**: Side by side comparison: Chu territory huge but with sparse dots representing population vs Qin territory smaller but dense with people and farms. Chu: big empty land. Qin: compact and efficient.
**Text**: 地大不精大部分是蛮荒之地
**Subtitle**: 人口稀少经济落后

## 03-content-system
**Layout**: illustration
**Visual**: Two palace scenes side by side: Chu court with nobles arguing, different factions pulling the king in different directions. Qin court with the king on high, officials bowing in order. Disorganized vs efficient.
**Text**: 楚国分封制贵族内耗严重
**Subtitle**: 秦国集权令行禁止

## 04-content-tribes
**Layout**: balanced
**Visual**: Chu king trying to rally soldiers from conquered southern tribes. The tribal soldiers look indifferent, some are turning away. Weak loyalty.
**Text**: 征服了南方部族但没有整合
**Subtitle**: 打仗时观望甚至倒戈

## 05-content-baiqi
**Layout**: action
**Visual**: Bai Qi's small but focused Qin army piercing through Chu territory like a hot knife through butter. Chu defense scattered and unable to concentrate. One focused blow breaks the giant.
**Text**: 白起几万秦军打穿楚国百万领土
**Subtitle**: 不是秦军太强是楚国太大

## 06-ending-lesson
**Layout**: sparse (ending)
**Visual**: Split comparison: small but solid Zhao fighting at Changping for 3 years vs huge but hollow Chu collapsing instantly. A lesson in the importance of concentration vs dispersion.
**Text**: 大小不重要能否集中力量才重要
