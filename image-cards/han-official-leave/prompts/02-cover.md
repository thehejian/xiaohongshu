Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Han dynasty scene. An official in mourning white robes kneeling before his parents' ancestral tablets, a government notice beside him granting him three years of paid leave. Sacred filial piety enforced by law — anyone who feasts during mourning loses his post and faces prison. Chinese title '"丁忧·守孝三年"' in calligraphic brush style.
