Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Han dynasty scene. A Han official happily walking home on his rest day carrying a bundle of clean clothes, wooden bucket for washing his hair, his family waiting at the door with a warm welcome. Street in Chang'an bustling with other officials heading home too. The joy of the five-day-work one-day-rest system. Chinese title '"休沐·洗白白"' in calligraphic brush style.
