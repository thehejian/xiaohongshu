Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Han dynasty scene. An official visiting his elderly parents in a distant town, riding a government courier horse with luggage, his elderly mother and father waiting at the gate with joy. A scroll showing travel leave calculated by distance. The warmth of the paid family visit leave system. Chinese title '"归宁省亲"' in calligraphic brush style.
