Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, vivid crimson and grey colors, dramatic composition, misty atmosphere. A tense night scene at a remote Han dynasty rural checkpoint (ting): a stern local officer (tingzhang) in rough official robes stands with a lantern raised high, blocking the path of a group of travelers in plain rustic clothes. The leader of the travelers, a calm middle-aged man with sharp eyes hidden beneath a commoner's cloth hat, stands his ground while his attendants behind him exchange nervous glances. A creaking wooden barrier, a dusty roadside hut, a crescent moon looming through mist. The dramatic moment an emperor in disguise is stopped and questioned by a lowly officer, tension and irony. Chinese title "亭长拦驾" in large bold calligraphic brush style.
