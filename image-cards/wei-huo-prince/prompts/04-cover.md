Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space. On the northern desert wasteland, wind and sand blot out the sky. Wei Qing orders the war chariots to form a circular defensive fort. Han cavalry spread out on both flanks, formations as solid as mountains. Xiongnu iron cavalry surge forward like a tide, but the Han line remains unmoved. Ancient Chinese people, Han dynasty military clothing. Chinese title "稳如泰山" in calligraphic brush style. Subtitle "七战七捷，全师而还". No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
