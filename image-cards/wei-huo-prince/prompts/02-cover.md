Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space. Before the gates of the Pingyang Marquis estate, young Wei Qing holds the reins of a horse, accompanying the princess's carriage. Chang'an spring streets bustle with activity. His posture is upright but his status is lowly — a horse groom. His eyes show endurance and determination. Ancient Chinese people, Han dynasty common and noble clothing. Chinese title "昔日骑奴" in calligraphic brush style. Subtitle "人奴之生，安得封侯". No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
