Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space. Three young men stand side by side — the center one calm and steady like a mountain (Wei Qing), the left one defiant and unruly (Huo Qubing), the right one composed and restrained (Huo Guang). Behind them, desert smoke and Chang'an palace towers overlap in the distance. Fierce northern wind billows their robes. Ancient Chinese people, Han dynasty clothing. Chinese title "私生子天团" in calligraphic brush style. Subtitle "卫青与霍去病". No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
