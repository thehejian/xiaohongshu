Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space. Beside Maoling mausoleum, Huo Qubing's tomb is shaped like the Qilian Mountains, blood-red sunset bathing the scene. In a corner of the painting, a faded silhouette shows Huo Guang standing solemnly before the Qilin Pavilion holding his official tablet — the war god and the statesman, one martial and one civil, both rising from the mud of Pingyang. Ancient Chinese men, Han dynasty clothing. Chinese title "祁连山冢·麒麟阁" in calligraphic brush style. Subtitle "从牧羊奴到废立天子". No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
