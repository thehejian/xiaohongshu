Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors. The siege of Baideng: Liu Bang trapped inside the fortress while Modu Chanyu's 400,000 Xiongnu cavalry encircle the city in four color-coded waves — white horses in the west, green in the east, black in the north, red in the south. Heavy snow falling, harsh winter atmosphere. Han soldiers with frostbitten fingers, stranded and helpless. Ancient Chinese people, period clothing. Chinese title "白登" in calligraphic brush style. Subtitle "挨打".
