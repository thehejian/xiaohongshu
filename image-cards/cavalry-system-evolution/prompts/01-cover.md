Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Three eras of Han cavalry evolution overlapping in one composition: Xiang Yu's cavalry charge at Pengcheng scattering the Han army, Guan Ying receiving the Chariot General seal from Emperor Liu Bang, Wei Qing's four-pillar military system represented by armoured chariots and cavalry formations. Ancient Chinese people, period clothing. Chinese title "骑兵进化" in calligraphic brush style. Ample whitespace.
