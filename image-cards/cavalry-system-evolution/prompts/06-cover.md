Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors. Han cavalry charging across the vast Mobei grassland, pursuing fleeing Xiongnu horsemen toward the northern horizon. Momentum of eighty years of reversal — from trapped at Baideng to dominating the steppe. Victory and power, the culmination of three generations. Ancient Chinese cavalry in Han armor. Chinese title "八十年" in calligraphic brush style. Subtitle "逆转".
