Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, abundant negative space, misty atmosphere. Left-right comparison composition. Left side (Han Wen Di): Emperor Wen Di in plain worn black Western Han hempen robe, waving his hand and saying "no need to build" — the scene of cancelling the dew terrace, frugality to the extreme. Right side (Tang Taizong): Tang Taizong in ochre-yellow round-collar Tang robe, pointing at an empty garden space and saying "we won't build here" to a minister beside him. The minister Wei Zheng standing next to him nodding with a knowing smile — Wei Zheng was famous for constantly advising Taizong to be frugal. Both emperors refusing luxury. Character labels: left "汉文帝刘恒", right "唐太宗李世民" and "魏征". Chinese title: 不约而同的节俭. Subtitle: 百金露台VS不建宫殿. Ancient Chinese people, Western Han emperor in worn plain black hempen robe, Tang emperor in ochre-yellow round-collar robe, Wei Zheng in purple Tang high-official robe (purple was highest Tang official rank). No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
