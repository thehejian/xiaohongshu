Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, abundant negative space, misty atmosphere. A grand panoramic scroll across time. Left half: Chang'an city of Western Han dynasty, ancient-style city walls, crisscrossing farm fields with farmers working — the scene of Wen-Jing prosperous age. Right half: Chang'an city of Tang dynasty, grander city walls, bustling market with foreign envoys and merchants — the scene of Zhenguan golden age. A flowing mist of ink connecting the two Chang'an cities across time. Above the mist float the words "文景之治" and "贞观之治". In the distant foreground, the side silhouette profiles of Han Wen Di (left) and Tang Taizong (right) face each other across the ages in a mutual salute. Chinese title: 隔空知音. Subtitle: 相隔八百年，同一个答案. Ancient Chinese people, only side silhouette profiles, no detailed clothing needed. No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
