Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, abundant negative space, misty atmosphere. Left-right comparison composition, both showing "ruling scenes". Left side (Han Wen Di): Emperor Wen Di in plain black Western Han hempen court robe, sitting at a simple desk writing on bamboo slips — scrolls inscribed with "三十税一" and "废除肉刑" before him, gentle expression, peaceful atmosphere. Right side (Tang Taizong): Tang Taizong in ochre-yellow round-collar Tang court robe, sitting in the court hall with Zhongshu and Menxia province officials presenting documents — he is revising the Tang Code (贞观律) and establishing government systems, focused expression, busier atmosphere. Above left side written "无为", above right side written "有为". Chinese title: 无为与有为. Subtitle: 两种方式，一样盛世. Ancient Chinese people, Western Han emperor in plain black hempen court robe, Tang emperor in ochre-yellow round-collar robe with putou, Tang officials in formal dark Tang robes. No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
