Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, abundant negative space, misty atmosphere. Symmetrical left-right composition. Left side: Han Emperor Wen Di Liu Heng standing at the edge of a wheat field, in plain black Western Han hempen robe, bending down to talk with an elderly farmer, holding a wheat stalk, kind expression. Right side: Tang Taizong Li Shiming wearing a Tang ochre-yellow round-collar robe, standing on the wall of Chang'an city, leaning on the railing overlooking the bustling market and commoners below, solemn expression. A calligraphic character "民" written in ink connecting the two scenes in the center. Chinese title: 以民为本. Subtitle: 为君之道，必先存百姓. Ancient Chinese people, Western Han emperor in plain black hempen robe, Tang emperor in ochre-yellow round-collar robe with putou. No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
