Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Two Han generals on horseback facing each other on a frontier battlefield, their banners whipping in the harsh wind. Left: Li Guang, bow in hand, cloak loose, troops scattered behind him in rugged disorder. Right: Cheng Bushi, formal and rigid, troops in perfect disciplined ranks behind him. Cold tension between two approaches to war. Ancient Chinese people, Li Guang in Han general armor with bow, Cheng Bushi in Han general armor straight-backed. Chinese title "简易与烦扰" in calligraphic brush style. No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
