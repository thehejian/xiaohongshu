Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Cheng Bushi's frontier camp at night—forbidding and impenetrable. Watchtowers blaze with torches, rows of tents aligned with military precision, armed patrols calling the hour. Officers study maps by lamplight in the command tent. The entire army braced, ready to repel any attack at a moment's notice. Grim discipline of a commander who never lost. Ancient Chinese people, Cheng Bushi in Han general armor inspecting defenses, officers and soldiers in armor. Chinese title "程不识军虽烦扰" in calligraphic brush style. No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
