Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. A dramatic battlefield after a storm. Left side: Cheng Bushi's intact banner rises above an orderly camp—undefeated record. Right side: broken arrows, abandoned weapons, Li Guang's flag fallen on the desert sand—the trace of his tragic end south of the Gobi in 119 BC. Ancient Chinese text fragments of military critics' judgments hang in the mist between them. Ancient Chinese people, Cheng Bushi in strong armor on one side, Li Guang's silhouette fading on the other. Chinese title "守兵与攻兵" in calligraphic brush style. No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
