Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. On the barren Hetao plain, thousands of laborers in coarse Han hemp clothing carry earth and stone to build a city wall. Some have collapsed from cold and hunger. Overseers in Han armor stand with whips. In the distance, the rammed-earth Great Wall and beacon towers stretch to the horizon. The sky is dark and oppressive. Ancient Chinese people, Han dynasty laborer and soldier clothing. No text labels, no English words. Chinese title "十万筑朔方" in calligraphic brush style. No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
