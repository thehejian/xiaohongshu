Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Emperor Wen lies on a sickbed with a painful ulcer. Deng Tong in attendant's clothing bends over, using his mouth to suck out the pus, expression obedient and devoted. On the other side, Crown Prince Qi in prince's robes stands by the bed, forced to also suck the pus—his face dark with disgust and resentment. Strong contrast between the two figures' expressions, undercurrents of tension. Ancient Chinese people, Han dynasty emperor, prince and attendant clothing. Chinese title "吮痈之辱" in calligraphic brush style. No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
