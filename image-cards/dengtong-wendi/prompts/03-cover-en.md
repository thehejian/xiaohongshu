Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. At the foot of Tong Mountain in Yandao, Shu commandery, Deng Tong in luxurious robes supervises workers. Furnace fires blaze, molten copper flows like water from molds, coins pile up like mountains. In the distance, caravans bearing "Deng's coins" travel in endless lines to all directions. Rich, intense composition with an underlying sense of impermanence. Ancient Chinese people, Han dynasty noble clothing. Chinese title "邓氏钱布天下" in calligraphic brush style. No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
