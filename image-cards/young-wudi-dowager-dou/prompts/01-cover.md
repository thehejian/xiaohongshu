Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. A young emperor in Han black-red ceremonial robes sitting alone at a low table with untouched food, his expression one of silent protest. In the background behind a screen, an elderly grand empress dowager in formal court robes holding court with officials. Han palace interior with rammed-earth walls and silk curtains. Chinese title "天子绝食" in calligraphic brush style, subtitle "少年武帝·窦后专权". No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
