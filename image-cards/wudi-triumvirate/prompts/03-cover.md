Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Gongsun Hong in the Han court, silent during debates, then speaking privately with Emperor Wu after court — the art of political subtlety. Behind him the image of the Imperial Academy taking shape.
