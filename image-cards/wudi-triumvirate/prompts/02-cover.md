Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Young Gongsun Hong herding pigs by the seashore, holding a bamboo scroll of the Spring and Autumn Annals while pigs scatter across the beach. Poor but determined to study. Ancient Chinese man in humble Han clothing. Chinese title "放猪" in calligraphic brush style. Subtitle "读春秋".
