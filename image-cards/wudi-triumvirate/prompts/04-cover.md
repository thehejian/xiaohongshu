Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Sang Hongyang as a thirteen-year-old prodigy serving in the Han court, his desk covered with abacus, copper coins, and tax registers. A mathematical genius who would become Emperor Wu's finance minister. Ancient Chinese youth in Han clothing. Chinese title "心算" in calligraphic brush style. Subtitle "入侍中".
