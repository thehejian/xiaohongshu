Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. On the Yellow River bank, Su Jian (father of Su Wu) directs over 100,000 laborers and garrison soldiers ramming earth to build the city wall. Dust rises as elm-wood tamping songs fill the air. In the distance, migrant families with elderly and children move toward the frontier. Rustic, massive infrastructure-building atmosphere. Ancient Chinese people, Han dynasty general, soldiers and laborers clothing. Chinese title "苏建筑朔方城" in calligraphic brush style. No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
