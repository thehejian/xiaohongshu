Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. At Jilusal Fortress west of Shuofang, Wang Zhaojun's carriage departs Han territory heading north. A pipa sounds softly as wind and sand blur the frontier scene. Huhanye Chanyu awaits her on the road ahead. Bittersweet beauty of a border fortress connecting the most famous peace marriage in history. Ancient Chinese people, Han dynasty palace lady and Xiongnu chanyu clothing. Chinese title "昭君出鸡鹿塞" in calligraphic brush style. No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
