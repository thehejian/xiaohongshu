Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Today's southern bank of the Yellow River, at Shilazhao in Hanggin Banner, Ordos—ruined city walls amid wild grass, no walls left, no tiles remain. A solitary sunset shines.
