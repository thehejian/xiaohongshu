Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Intense battlefield on the Mongolian steppe — five Xiongnu cavalry factions charging at each other, arrows darkening the sky, fallen warriors and horses everywhere, dust clouds and chaos. In stark contrast, the distant southern Han border is completely calm and peaceful. Chinese title '"五单于血战"' in calligraphic brush style.
