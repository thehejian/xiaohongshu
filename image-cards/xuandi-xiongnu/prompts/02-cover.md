Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Han dynasty palace study. Emperor Xuan sitting calmly at his desk issuing three edicts — border grain储备, Huhanye seal of recognition, and Zheng Ji's Western Regions diversion — represented as three scrolls on the desk. No military urgency, only the quiet confidence of strategic wisdom. Chinese title '"三棋·不战"' in calligraphic brush style.
