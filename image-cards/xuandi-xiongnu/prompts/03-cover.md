Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Han dynasty period setting. Zhao Chongguo's soldiers farming and drilling at the Huangshui frontier garrison, and in the far distance the Western Regions Protectorate banner rises over Wulei City — two pincers forming a giant encirclement of the northern steppe. The dual strategy of economic colonization and strategic outflanking. Chinese title '"屯田·合围"' in calligraphic brush style.
