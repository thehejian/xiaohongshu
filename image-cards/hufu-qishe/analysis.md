# Content Analysis: 胡服骑射

## Content Type
Military reform story

## Topic
King Wuling of Zhao's reform — adopting Hu (nomadic) clothing and horseback archery to transform Zhao's military

## Key Points
- Zhao surrounded by enemies, losing battles
- Traditional long robes made horseback fighting impossible
- Hu people: short jackets, trousers, boots, horseback archery
- Reform: 胡服 (clothing) + 骑射 (cavalry archery)
- Fierce opposition from conservative nobles
- King Wuling: "法古之学，不足以制今"
- Result: Zhao became military superpower, cavalry changed Chinese warfare
- Tragic death: starved in palace coup

## Audience
Chinese military history fans
