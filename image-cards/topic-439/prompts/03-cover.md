Ink wash painting with subtle color tints, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, delicate pale colors peeking through, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Liao dynasty, a long column of Khitan refugees with covered carts, camels and armored riders migrating westward across a vast Central Asian desert, banners leaning in the wind, distant snow mountains on the horizon, setting sun casting long shadows. no text, no calligraphy, no writing.
