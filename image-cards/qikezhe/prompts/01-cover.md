Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. A column of Han dynasty conscripts in ragged clothing being marched westward across the Gobi desert, guarded by armed soldiers. Among them: men in chains (criminals), men with merchant hats, men in simple wedding robes (concubine husbands). Dusty road, distant mountains. Chinese title "七科谪" in calligraphic brush style, subtitle "罪犯·赘婿·商贾". No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
