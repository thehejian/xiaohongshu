Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Two Han-dynasty officials in formal court robes walking side by side through a palace corridor, representing the Jin and Zhang families who served as high officials for seven generations. Palace architecture with rammed-earth walls and tiled roofs. Chinese title "金张·齐名" in calligraphic brush style, subtitle "七世珥汉貂". No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
