Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Inside the Chang'an Imperial Academy lecture hall, Erudite students sit reciting classics, bamboo scrolls like a forest. In one corner, commoner Gongsun Hong rises directly from the fields into the court to be enfeoffed as Marquis of Pingjin—glory and simplicity coexist. Elegant upward composition symbolizing the opening of a channel for commoners. Ancient Chinese people, Han dynasty scholars and chancellor clothing. Chinese title "太学·白衣丞相" in calligraphic brush style. No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
