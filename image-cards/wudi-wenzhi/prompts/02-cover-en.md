Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. In Weiyang Palace, Emperor Wu sits solemnly as Dong Zhongshu kneels presenting the "Three Essays on Heaven and Man" on bamboo slips. On the desk, the phrase "Spring and Autumn Annals Great Unity" is conspicuous. On both sides, representatives of the Hundred Schools (Daoist, Legalist, Mohist) gradually fade into mist. Solemn composition symbolizing the unification of thought. Ancient Chinese people, Han dynasty emperor and scholar clothing. Chinese title "独尊儒术" in calligraphic brush style. No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
