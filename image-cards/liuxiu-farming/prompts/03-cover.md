Ink wash painting style on light rice paper, flowing ink strokes, sparse composition with generous negative space, subtle crimson and grey palette, misty atmosphere, consistent with the earlier panels. A commanding presence on the wall of a besieged ancient city: a tall Western Han commander in polished leather armor with his hand resting on a long sword at his hip stands in a proud, broad-shouldered stance, cloak whipping in the wind, chin slightly raised, eyes narrowed and piercing as he gazes at an overwhelming sea of enemy banners stretching to the horizon. His bearing is mighty, unshakable, like a great peak. Behind him loyal soldiers stand firm with raised spears. No gore, no violence. Chinese calligraphic title inscription: "稳如老农". No other text.
