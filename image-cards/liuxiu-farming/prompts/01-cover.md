Ink wash painting style on light rice paper, flowing ink strokes, sparse composition with generous negative space, subtle crimson and grey palette, misty golden morning atmosphere. A young man with authentic Western Han hairstyle — hair tied into a topknot (束发髻) and wrapped in a plain cloth headband (帻), no loose flowing hair — wearing a coarse short-sleeved work jacket (短褐) with trousers, trousers rolled up at the calves, works a wooden plow pulled by an ox in a lush rice paddy field. Rolling farmland stretches behind him toward misty distant mountains. His posture is practiced and easy, a true farmer. Warm, earthy, hopeful mood. Chinese calligraphic title inscription: "皇帝曾是种田郎".
