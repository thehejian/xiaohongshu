Ink wash painting style on light rice paper, flowing ink strokes, sparse composition with generous negative space, subtle crimson and grey palette, misty atmosphere, consistent with the first panel. An exaggeratedly mocking courtyard scene: an energetic warrior in Han armor doubles over laughing uncontrollably, one hand slapping his own knee, the other pointing straight at a young man in simple farmer's clothes hoeing in the field beside them, his mouth wide open in a great booming laugh with tears of laughter. Two bystanders nearby also chuckle and point, joining the ridicule. In the foreground, a hoe and sickle lie beside a weapons rack of spears and swords, contrasting farming and warfare. Loud, derisive, boisterous, comical mood. Chinese calligraphic title inscription: "刘老三·种地去". No other text.
