# Content Analysis: 张仪诈楚

## Content Type
Historical trickery / strategic deception

## Topic
Zhang Yi's famous deception of Chu — promising 600 li of land to break the Qi-Chu alliance, then only delivering 6 li

## Key Points
- Zhang Yi needed to break Qi-Chu alliance before Qin could attack Qi
- Promised Chu king 600 li + marriage alliance
- Chu king broke with Qi, sent envoy
- Zhang Yi feigned injury, stalled
- Chu king further insulted Qi to prove loyalty
- Qi allied with Qin, Zhang Yi revealed: 6 li not 600
- Chu attacked, lost 80,000 soldiers, never recovered
