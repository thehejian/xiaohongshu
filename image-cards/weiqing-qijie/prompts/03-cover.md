Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors. Wei Qing leading his army in a grand military campaign across the Yellow River bend: thousands of Han cavalry in formation marching across the vast plain, Wei Qing at the head on horseback, tall and commanding, pointing forward with his sword. In the distance the Great Wall and newly built Shuofang city walls. War drums and banners filling the scene. Ancient Chinese people, period clothing. Chinese title "河南·筑朔方" in calligraphic brush style. No heavy outlines.
