Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. A panoramic view of a vast mausoleum construction site in Han dynasty China: thousands of workers moving earth, stone being carved, rammed-earth walls rising, wooden scaffolding, carts carrying huge stones. The scale is colossal, stretching into the mist. Chinese title "五十三载修陵" in calligraphic brush style, subtitle "即位次年始·五十四年毕". No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
