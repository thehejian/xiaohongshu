Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Inside Weiyang Palace, the emperor holds an edict for reducing feudal states. On his desk lies a map of the kingdoms, several territories already crossed out in red ink. Below the throne, a minister kneels presenting a bamboo-slip memorial, his expression resolute. In the distance beyond the palace, faint smoke signals of the seven rebel kingdoms are visible. Ancient Chinese people, Han dynasty imperial and court clothing. No text labels, no English words. Chinese title "削藩策" in calligraphic brush style. No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
