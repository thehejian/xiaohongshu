Ink wash painting with subtle color tints, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, delicate pale colors peeking through, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. A late Qing scene by a riverbank: a scholar in a dark robe sits on a wooden bench holding a letter, his expression troubled, in the far distance a line of steamships with black smoke on the water and a city skyline with old city walls and modern buildings coexisting. no text, no calligraphy, no writing.
