Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Han dynasty shenyi robe with crossed-collar right closure (交领右衽), traditional topknot with guan headpiece (冠), palace with rammed-earth walls and tiled roof, low tables and floor mats. The inner court seal casting a long shadow that grows into Wang Mang's usurpation. Chinese title "内朝黑洞" in calligraphic brush style.
