Ink wash painting style on light rice paper, flowing ink strokes, sparse composition with generous negative space, subtle crimson and grey palette, misty atmosphere. A Western Han emperor in dark imperial robes with a flat-topped crown (no dragon motifs) sits at a grand desk, surrounded by scattered ancient bamboo-slip scrolls of the Zhouli (周礼), crossed-out imperial edicts, and assorted coins — turtle shells, cowrie shells, and cloth-shaped bronze money — piling chaotically on the table and floor. His expression is self-assured and absorbed. Ironic, chaotic, doomed mood. Chinese calligraphic title inscription: "王莽·改制". No other text.
