Ink wash painting style on light rice paper, flowing ink strokes, sparse composition with generous negative space, subtle crimson and grey palette, misty atmosphere, consistent with the first panel. A Western Han marketplace scene: a smoldering furnace melts a heap of round bronze wu-zhu coins (五铢钱) into slag, while ordinary townspeople hold up turtle shells and cowrie shells as replacements, their faces bewildered and helpless. A merchant stares at his emptied money pouch. Bewildered, distressed, ironic mood. No gore. Chinese calligraphic title inscription: "钱法大乱". No other text.
