Ink wash painting style on light rice paper, flowing ink strokes, sparse composition with generous negative space, subtle crimson and grey palette, misty atmosphere. A tranquil wide landscape at dusk: red banners flutter on a distant hilltop and green banners wave among forested mountains far behind, an empty country road crossing the foreground with a few scattered farmhouses, under a vast overcast grey sky. Serene, vast, slightly melancholic atmosphere. No figures, no smoke, no violence, no conflict. Chinese calligraphic title inscription: "王朝·末年". No other text.
