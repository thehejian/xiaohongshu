Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Split composition: left side shows a Han dynasty teenage boy in patched commoner clothes watching cockfights on a dusty Chang'an street; right side shows a Tang dynasty young man in plain palace robes pretending to be dull, eyes subtly alert. Two grassroots emperors side by side. Chinese title '"两位草根皇帝"' in calligraphic brush style.
