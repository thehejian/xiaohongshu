Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Tang dynasty palace setting with round-collared robes and winged guan. Li Chen (Tang Xuanzong) suddenly removing a foolish mask on his coronation day, eyes sharp as an eagle, stunned eunuchs and officials around him. The dramatic transformation from fool to emperor. Chinese title '"装傻·反杀"' in calligraphic brush style.
