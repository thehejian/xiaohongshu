Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Beneath the Qilian Mountains, Huo Qubing's cavalry gallops across the grassland. The four commandery cities of Wuwei, Zhangye, Jiuquan and Dunhuang rise in sequence, the Hexi Corridor extending like an arm toward the west. Only male figures—Han dynasty cavalry and Xiongnu male herders. No women or female figures anywhere. Chinese title "河西四郡" in calligraphic brush style. No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
