Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Tower-ship General Yang Pu and Fubo General Lu Bode advance by water and land. The city of Panyu surrenders. Han nine-commandery banners rise over the miasma-shrouded Lingnan region. Envoys from Yelang and Dian kingdoms come to court, the Dian king's seal glittering gold. Vast, expansive scene of the southern frontier's first integration. Ancient Chinese people, Han dynasty general, Yue and Dian people clothing. Chinese title "南平百越" in calligraphic brush style. No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
