Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. On the Bohai coast, tower-ships break through the waves as Yang Pu and Xun Zhi attack Wangxian City by water and land. Wei Youqu is defeated. On the northern Korean peninsula, Han banners of the four commanderies—Lelang, Lintun, Xuantu, Zhenfan—rise. Vast, trackless scene of the eastern frontier's first entry into the imperial map. Ancient Chinese people, Han dynasty soldiers and Korean people clothing. Chinese title "汉四郡·东并朝鲜" in calligraphic brush style. No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
