Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. A solitary traveler in the vast desert—Zhang Qian holds the Han imperial tally, robes weathered by wind and dust. Beside him, Ganfu leads a horse. After ten years of captivity the tally's tail-tassels remain intact. In the background, exotic cities of the Western Regions (Dayuan, Daxia, Anxi) and camel caravans mark the newly opened Silk Road. Ancient Chinese people, Han dynasty envoy and Western Region male travelers only. No women or female figures. Chinese title "张骞凿空" in calligraphic brush style. No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
