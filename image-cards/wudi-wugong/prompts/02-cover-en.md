Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. On the snowy northern steppe, young general Huo Qubing (age 21), with a youthful heroic face, leads armored cavalry up Mount Langjuxu, building an altar for heaven worship and raising battle flags. Behind him, thousands of riders surge like tides, banners blotting the sun. In the distance, the Chanyu's court smoke dissipates. Majestic, martial composition. Ancient Chinese people, Han dynasty general and cavalry clothing. Chinese title "封狼居胥" in calligraphic brush style. No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
