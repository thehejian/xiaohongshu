Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. The suffocating Gao Min (property confession) scene. In a marketplace square, Yang Ke stands on a high platform holding an imperial tally as "Gao Min Envoy." Neighbors point accusing fingers at middle-class merchant families. The accused merchant's homes are sealed, fields measured and confiscated, servants lined up and marched away by officials, coins and silk carried into government warehouses. Mountains of confiscated wealth pile up. Despair of ruined middle-class families pervades the scene. Ancient Chinese people, Han officials in official robes, informants and merchants in civilian Han clothing. Chinese title "杨可告缗·中家皆破" in calligraphic brush style. No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
