Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Government salt and iron monopoly scene. Center: official Salt and Iron Bureau building with a sign, government officials with seals sealing private salt pans and iron foundries. Left: private salt boiling stoves extinguished and sealed. Right: private iron workshop closed, craftsmen standing outside dejectedly. Background: streams of salt-iron revenue flowing into the imperial treasury like a river, symbolizing wealth being drained from the people. Ancient Chinese people, Han officials in black-trimmed red official robes, craftsmen in hemp short garments. Chinese title "盐铁收归国营" in calligraphic brush style. No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
