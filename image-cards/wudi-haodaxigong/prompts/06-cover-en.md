Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. A solemn scene from Chang'an in 91 BC. In the foreground, official Jiang Chong stands in a courtyard with earth dug up around him. Guards search the grounds. Crown Prince Liu Ju on horseback with a few soldiers in the palace avenue. In the distance at Lake County, a lone tree stands by a small hut. Empress Wei Zifu sits quietly in her chambers. Faint outline of a memorial tower on the horizon. Melancholic, subdued atmosphere. Ancient Chinese people, Han dynasty crown prince in ceremonial armor, officials in robes, palace ladies in Han court dress. Chinese title "巫蛊之祸·太子冤死" in calligraphic brush style. No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
