Han dynasty soldiers in iron lamellar armor sitting on the grassland, eating millet from wooden bowls and dried meat, garlic cloves beside them. Horses tethered nearby. Distant camp tents and banners. Chinese title "汉军军粮" in calligraphic brush style, subtitle "粟饭·肉脯·蒜". No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
