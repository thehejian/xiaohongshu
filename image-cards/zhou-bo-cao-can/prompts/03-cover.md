Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, abundant negative space, misty atmosphere. Zhou Bo in chains inside a dark prison cell, a pitiful figure. He kneels on the dirty floor, trembling hands pushing a pile of gold towards a prison guard. The guard stands over him with contempt, casually holding a wooden slip with hidden writing. The wall shadow shows shackles. Zhou Bo's face shows complete despair — the general who commanded a million troops now begging a jailer for survival tips. Chinese title: 狱吏之贵. Subtitle: 吾尝将百万军，然安知狱吏之贵乎.
