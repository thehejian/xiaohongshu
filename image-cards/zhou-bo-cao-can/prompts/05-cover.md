Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, abundant negative space, misty atmosphere. Cao Can in his Qi chancellor office, dressed in plain Han scholar robe, sitting calmly holding a small Han lacquer ear cup (漆耳杯), listening to an old Daoist master (盖公) who sits across from him explaining Huang-Lao philosophy. The scene is relaxed and scholarly. Behind them through the window, the peaceful streets of Linzi city. A few books and scrolls around. Chinese title: 拜师盖公. Subtitle: 放下刀以后，他学会的第一件事.
