Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, abundant negative space, misty atmosphere. The imperial chamber: Empress Dowager Bo angrily grabbing her headscarf (冒絮) and throwing it at Emperor Wen's face, shouting: "周勃当年掌玉玺、统北军都没反，现在守个小县城你要杀他？" Emperor Wen bows his head apologetically, looking ashamed. Two maids behind the empress dowager look shocked. The scene captures the raw mother-son confrontation that saved Zhou Bo's life. Chinese title: 薄太后掷巾. Subtitle: 抓头巾砸皇帝——周勃就这么被救了. Ancient Chinese people, Western Han, Empress Dowager Bo in Han court robe, Emperor Wen in plain black hempen court robe. No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
