Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. A simplified outline map of China in brush style, four red dots marking the locations: Yunmeng Hubei, Liye Hunan, Jiuquan/Ejin Banner Gansu, and Jiangling Hubei. Each red dot annotated with small text indicating the excavation year and number of slips. Below the map, four rolls of bamboo slips slowly unfurling, the fragments gradually assembling like puzzle pieces rising upward to form a powerful calligraphic character "法" (law) in the upper area. The overall composition resembling a scholar's hand-drawn geographical notes. No human figures. Large calligraphic title at center: 四大简牍. Subtitle: 四块拼图拼出秦汉法制. No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
