Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. A Han dynasty beacon tower ruins on the Gobi desert at sunset, a garrison soldier crouching on the sandy ground holding a fragment of a bamboo slip in his hand, gazing at it. The Great Wall of Han winding like a ribbon in the distance, several beacon towers connected in a line. Broken bamboo slips and gravel scattered on the ground. The soldier wearing Han dynasty garrison armor and short robe, weathered face with vicissitudes. A dark red glow in the sky like blood at the horizon. Large calligraphic title: 居延汉简. Subtitle: 烽燧下的法律. Ancient Chinese people, Han dynasty garrison soldier clothing. No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
