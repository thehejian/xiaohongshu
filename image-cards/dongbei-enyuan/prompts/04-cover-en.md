Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. 342 AD, Former Yan cavalry under Murong Huang storms Goguryeo's Wandu Mountain Fortress. Xianbei cavalry in iron armor and helmets with long lances (长槊) and bows charge through the stone city gates. Goguryeo defenders resist from the walls. Palace buildings burn inside the city. The tomb of King Micheon is broken open, coffins scattered. Civilians are captured and led away. Late winter atmosphere, bleak and mournful. Ancient Chinese people, Sixteen Kingdoms Xianbei and Goguryeo period military clothing. Chinese title "慕容皝破丸都" in calligraphic brush style. No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
