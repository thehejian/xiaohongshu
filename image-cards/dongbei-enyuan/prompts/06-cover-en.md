Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. The destiny loop of two millennia. Between the White Mountains and Black Waters of the northeast, two eras of Jurchen power. Left foreground: Wanyan Aguda leads Jin cavalry charge, Jurchen iron-armored warriors with leather helmets, composite bows and wolf-tooth maces. Right mid-ground: Nurhaci's Jianzhou Jurchen Eight Banner army assembles in padded armor and iron helmets, holding long spears and early firearms, Eight Banner flags flutter. Both cavalry lines point toward the distant Central Plains city. Mist and mountains connect the two eras into one timeline. Echo of Han banners creates a sense of fate. Ancient Chinese people, Jin dynasty and Ming dynasty Manchu military clothing. Chinese title "金与清·东北入关" in calligraphic brush style. No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
