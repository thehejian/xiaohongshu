Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Two types of fortress stand in the northeast mountains. Left: a Han commandery city with square rammed-earth walls and Han-style official buildings, Han soldiers patrol in lamellar armor. Right on a hill: a Goguryeo mountain fortress built of stone, Goguryeo warriors in mixed iron lamellar and leather armor carrying ring-pommel swords and bows. Between them, a valley suggests coming conflict. Quiet tension before the storm. Ancient Chinese people, Han dynasty and Goguryeo period clothing. Chinese title "汉四郡·高句丽初兴" in calligraphic brush style. No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
