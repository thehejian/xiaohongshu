Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. 668 AD, Tang forces storm Pyongyang. Tang soldiers in Tang dynasty Bright Armor (明光铠) with polished metal chest plates hold Tang banners and long-blade Mo Dao (陌刀). Xue Rengui in white robe and silver armor charges at the front. Li Ji commands from the main banner position. Goguryeo defenders on the stone city walls shoot arrows down. Tang flags rise over the battlements as the Protectorate of Andong is established. Victorious yet solemn moment as a 700-year kingdom falls. Ancient Chinese people, Tang dynasty and Goguryeo period military clothing. Chinese title "唐灭高句丽" in calligraphic brush style. No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
