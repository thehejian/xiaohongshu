Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. 238 AD, the fall of Xiangping City. Wei soldiers in Three Kingdoms Wei-style lamellar armor break through the city gate carrying Wei banners. Gongsun Yuan flees on horseback with a few hundred cavalry but is cut down. Sima Yi watches from a war chariot, wearing a general's cape. In the distance, a Jingguan (tower of enemy corpses piled with earth) stands stark. The city tower burns, smoke rises over the rammed-earth Liaodong walls. Grim, brutal atmosphere. Ancient Chinese people, Three Kingdoms Wei period military clothing. Chinese title "司马懿灭公孙渊" in calligraphic brush style. No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
