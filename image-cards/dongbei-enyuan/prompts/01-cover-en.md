Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Left side: Han dynasty fleet under Admiral Yang Pu crossing the Bohai Sea with warships, soldiers in Han black iron lamellar armor (札甲) holding ring-pommel swords (环首刀) and crossbows. Right side: General Xun Zhi leading Han troops out of Liaodong on horseback, soldiers holding halberds (戈) and shields, Han banners flying. Center: Wangxian City under siege, rammed-earth walls breached, Wei Youqu the last king of Wei-Man Joseon being killed inside. Grand two-pronged invasion scene. Ancient Chinese people, Han dynasty military clothing. Chinese title "武帝东并朝鲜" in calligraphic brush style. No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
