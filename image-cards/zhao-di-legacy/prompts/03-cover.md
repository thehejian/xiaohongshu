Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. A Han dynasty scene showing a young emperor restraining himself: he sits in a reading room with scrolls and documents, a minister (霍光) respectfully presenting reports. The emperor listens without interrupting, demonstrating restraint. Peaceful fields and contented farmers visible through the window, symbolizing the results of non-interference. Chinese title "克制·不折腾" in calligraphic brush style, subtitle "放手让贤·十三年如一日". No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
