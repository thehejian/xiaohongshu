Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. The Siege of Baideng Mountain. Han emperor Liu Bang's infantry and chariot formation is encircled by layer upon layer of Xiongnu cavalry—four hundred thousand riders on the frozen plain. The stark contrast between Han foot soldiers and the endless sea of steppe horsemen shows the technological gap. The starting point of Han cavalry evolution. Ancient Chinese people, Liu Bang in royal robes within chariot formation, Han soldiers with spears, Xiongnu riders in fur on horseback. Chinese title "白登之围·步车劣势" in calligraphic brush style. No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
