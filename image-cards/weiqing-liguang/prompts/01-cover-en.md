Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Two Han generals contrasted in one frame. Left side: Li Guang draws his bow, arrow piercing into a stone ridge (the arrow-in-stone legend), soldiers around him admire his archery. Right side: Wei Qing sits calmly on horseback before a large army formation, steady and commanding. One embodies personal bravery, the other strategic command. Their gazes directed outward in the same direction but in different eras. Ancient Chinese people, Li Guang in battle armor drawing bow, Wei Qing in general's armor on horseback. Chinese title "飞将军与大将军" in calligraphic brush style. No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
