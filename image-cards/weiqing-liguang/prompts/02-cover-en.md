Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Li Guang feigns death lying in a Xiongnu tent after capture. He suddenly leaps onto a young Xiongnu rider's horse, grabbing the rider's bow, and gallops south across a snowy wilderness pursued by hundreds of Xiongnu horsemen. A dramatic escape through blizzard—one man against the vast northern plain. Ancient Chinese people, Li Guang in Han battle armor, Xiongnu soldiers in fur clothing on horseback. Chinese title "飞将军佯死脱归" in calligraphic brush style. No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
