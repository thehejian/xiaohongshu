Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. The Mobei battle scene at dusk. Wei Qing orders Wugang chariots to form a circular fortress. From the gaps, five thousand Han cavalry charge out like a tide. Dust storms swirl in the twilight as Han wings encircle the Xiongnu chanyu's camp. Chariot defense and cavalry offense working in precise coordination—a new era of warfare on the steppe. Ancient Chinese people, Wei Qing in general armor directing the battle, Han soldiers in armor with ring-pommel swords. Chinese title "武刚车阵·车骑协同" in calligraphic brush style. No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
