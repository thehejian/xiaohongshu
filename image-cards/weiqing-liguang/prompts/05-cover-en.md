Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Huo Qubing leads light cavalry on a lightning raid two thousand li deep into enemy territory—no supply wagons, living off the land. He ascends Mount Langjuxu, erecting a stone monument for the mountain ceremony. Behind him, Han light cavalry race across the grassland, transformed from defensive troops into the empire's most lethal strike force. Ancient Chinese people, Huo Qubing in light general armor, Han cavalry in light armor galloping. Chinese title "霍去病·轻骑闪电奔袭" in calligraphic brush style. No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
