Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Han dynasty tavern by the Wei River. Emperor Cheng in commoner clothes in a chaotic brawl with street thugs, Zhang Fang pulling him away, spilled wine and scattered tables. A night of imperial debauchery ending in a black eye. Chinese title '"天子斗殴"' in calligraphic brush style.
