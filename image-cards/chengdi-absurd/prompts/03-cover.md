Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Han dynasty palace hall. Emperor Cheng weeping uncontrollably on his throne as Zhang Fang is escorted away by guards, imperial courtiers looking away in embarrassment. A ruler more heartbroken over losing his favorite than over the dying empire. Chinese title '"哭送男闺蜜"' in calligraphic brush style.
