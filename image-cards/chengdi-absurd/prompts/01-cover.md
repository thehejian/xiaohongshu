Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Han dynasty scene. Two men in commoner clothes climbing over the palace wall at night under moonlight — one carrying the other on his back, sneaking back in after a night of debauchery. A lone eunuch waiting nervously by the side door. Chinese title '"微服夜游"' in calligraphic brush style.
