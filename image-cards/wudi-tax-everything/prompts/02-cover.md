Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. A Han dynasty peasant couple in ragged clothing holding a small child, a tax official with a ledger pointing at the child, demanding payment. Simple thatched cottage, a few chickens in the yard. The parents looking distressed. Chinese title "三岁口赋" in calligraphic brush style, subtitle "三岁孩童·亦纳税". No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
