Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. A Han dynasty tax collector at a desk with scrolls and coins, a long queue of commoners including a mother with a baby, a farmer with a cart, a fisherman with a boat. Various tax items labeled with wooden slips. Chinese title "万物皆税" in calligraphic brush style, subtitle "口赋·车船算". No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
