Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. A Han dynasty river port with fishing boats and merchant ships, a tax official collecting fees from boat owners. An ox-cart on the dock being inspected. Merchants counting coins reluctantly. Chinese title "车船算" in calligraphic brush style, subtitle "商车倍·船亦税". No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
