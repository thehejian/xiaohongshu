Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. A young Han dynasty general in black lamellar armor (xuanjia) and red battle robe stands on an open grassland, with distant misty Qilian Mountains behind him. His face is young and calm, gazing into the distance, wind blowing his robe. Upper two-thirds of the frame is sky and distant mountains, the figure in the lower right corner. Large areas of negative space. Ancient Chinese people, period clothing. Chinese title "霍去病" in calligraphic brush style. Subtitle "二十四岁的绝响". No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
