Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Han dynasty palace hall with rammed-earth walls and floor mats. Huhanye Chanyu in fur-trimmed Xiongnu robe kneeling before Emperor Xuan on throne, piles of gold and silk being presented as imperial gifts between them. The moment conveys final peace after 150 years of war. Chinese title '"单于朝拜甘露年"' in calligraphic brush style.
