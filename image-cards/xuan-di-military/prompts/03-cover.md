Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Han dynasty period setting. Elderly general Zhao Chongguo in simple military robe inspecting farmland along the Huangshui River, Han soldiers farming and drilling, Qiang tribal envoys watching peacefully from a distance. Mountains in background. Chinese title '"屯田平羌"' in calligraphic brush style.
