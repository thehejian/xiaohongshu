Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. The Xiongnu Chanyu Modu sitting in his tent, looking thoughtful and calculating. A map of the battlefield before him, generals waiting. Outside the tent, two faraway horizon where allied armies have not yet appeared. Xiongnu people, leather and fur clothing. Chinese title 冒顿盘算 in calligraphic brush style. Subtitle 盟友未至，汉军主力已逼近. No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
