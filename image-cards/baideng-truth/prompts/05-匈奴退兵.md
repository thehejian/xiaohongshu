Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. The Xiongnu army withdrawing from Baideng mountain at dawn, opening a path. Han soldiers on the mountain watching cautiously. Misty morning light, heavy snow covering the battlefield. Ancient Chinese and Xiongnu people. Chinese title 绝处逢生 in calligraphic brush style. Subtitle 冒顿撤围，让出一条生路. No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
