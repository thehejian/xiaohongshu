Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Han dynasty workshop scene. A skilled jade craftsman assembling a gold-thread jade burial suit, thousands of polished jade pieces laid out on a table, golden threads connecting them, the shimmering suit on display. The obsession and craftsmanship behind the most extravagant burial garment in history. Chinese title '"金缕玉衣·十年一衣"' in calligraphic brush style.
