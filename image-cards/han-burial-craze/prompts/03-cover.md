Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Night scene. Tomb raiders with ropes and lanterns breaking into a Han dynasty burial mound under moonlight, breaking through the sealed gate, gold and jade glimpsed inside. A dark figure handing treasures to an accomplice. The industrial-scale tomb robbing of Han dynasty extravagance. Chinese title '"盗墓·产业链"' in calligraphic brush style. No blood, no violence.
