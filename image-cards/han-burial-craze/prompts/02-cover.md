Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Han dynasty tomb interior modeled after a palace — a grand underground chamber filled with bronze vessels, lacquerware, hundreds of pottery jars of food, gold and silk treasures, a model of a house. The belief in living after death ('shi si ru sheng'). Chinese title '"事死如生·地下皇宫"' in calligraphic brush style.
