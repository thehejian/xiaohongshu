Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. On a country road, young woman Wang Zhi kneels bidding farewell to her former husband Jin Wangsun and their young daughter, tears streaming down her face. Behind her, elderly Zang Er — granddaughter of King Zang Tu of Yan — grips her daughter's wrist with piercing eyes, forcing her to leave. In the distance, palace towers are faintly visible through the mist. Ancient Chinese people, Han dynasty clothing. Chinese title "再婚的皇后" in calligraphic brush style. Subtitle "嫁过人生过孩子，最后做了太后". No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
