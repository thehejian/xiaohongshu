Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Princess Guantao holds young Liu Zhi (piglet name) on her lap in a courtyard. Liu Zhi points a little finger into the distance, innocently saying the words that would become the legend of a golden house for Ajiao. Wang Zhi stands nearby, smiling knowingly. On a pillar, a faint piglet carving hints at the child's nickname. Ancient Chinese people, Han dynasty court clothing. Chinese title "金屋藏娇" in calligraphic brush style. Subtitle "一头小猪，一句童言，改变历史". No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
