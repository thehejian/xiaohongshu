Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. In a deep palace courtyard, Princess Guantao in splendid robes storms away in anger, her sleeves billowing. Behind her, Lady Li stands in the doorway, her expression cold and stubborn, one hand already pulling the door half-closed. The marriage alliance is broken off. Ancient Chinese people, Han dynasty court clothing. Chinese title "提亲被拒" in calligraphic brush style. Subtitle "栗姬一怒，太子位易主". No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
