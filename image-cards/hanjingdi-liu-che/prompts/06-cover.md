Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. In the vast empty hall, young Liu Che kneels to receive the crown prince edict, holding the scroll in both hands with solemn expression. Three female silhouettes are cast on the curtain — Princess Guantao with flowing sleeves, steady and shrewd Wang Zhi, and elderly Zang Er leaning on a staff. In a dark corner, a faint shadow of the starved Liu Rong can be seen. Ancient Chinese people, Han dynasty court clothing. Chinese title "十子逆袭" in calligraphic brush style. Subtitle "外婆逼母再婚，小猪终成龙". No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
