Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. A wide panoramic view of a Han dynasty imperial procession heading toward a vast flooded landscape: an emperor in a chariot leading thousands of soldiers and officials, banners waving, crossing a muddy plain toward the distant Yellow River breach. Flooded fields and submerged villages visible in the background. Chinese title "帝亲·赴决口" in calligraphic brush style, subtitle "数万百官·共赴黄河". No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
