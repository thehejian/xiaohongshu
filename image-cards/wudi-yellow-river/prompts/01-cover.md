Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. A dramatic Han dynasty scene at a Yellow River breach: an emperor in plain work clothes carrying a bundle of brushwood on his shoulder, standing amid thousands of workers at the riverbank. Mist rises from the turbulent water. Imperial officials in formal robes also carry earth and wood, following the emperor's lead. Chinese title "武帝治河" in calligraphic brush style, subtitle "瓠子堵口·帝亲扛土". No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
