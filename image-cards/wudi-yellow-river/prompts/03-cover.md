Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. A Han dynasty riverbank scene: an emperor, now in simple clothes, standing at the water's edge throwing a bundle of wood into the turbulent river. Behind him, high-ranking officials in ministerial robes also carry brushwood and baskets of earth. The scale of the flood-fighting effort is immense, with thousands of figures stretching along the river. Chinese title "百官负薪·帝扛土" in calligraphic brush style, subtitle "君臣同劳·合龙在即". No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
