Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. In Yunyang Palace, Lady Gouyi kneels removing her hairpins and kowtowing. Emperor Wu stands with his back turned, robes sharp and murderous. Palace maids hold their breath and retreat. Outside the hall, fierce wind stirs dust. The scene is utterly still and ice-cold—the most piercing moment of the entire narrative. Ancient Chinese people, Han dynasty emperor, consort and palace maid clothing. Chinese title "立子杀母" in calligraphic brush style. No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
