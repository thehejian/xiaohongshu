Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Five hazy figures stand side by side—Dou Ying, Tian Fen, Wei Qing, Huo Qubing, and Li Guangli—all in marquis and general court robes, each with shadows on their faces and faint blood-like ripples at their feet. Background shows the cold moon over Weiyang Palace. Ancient Chinese people, Han dynasty marquis and general clothing. Chinese title "外戚，没一个善终" in calligraphic brush style. No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
