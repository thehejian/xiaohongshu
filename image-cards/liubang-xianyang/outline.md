# Outline

## 01-cover-two-paths
**Visual**: Two paths on a map. Xiang Yu's direct hard path through Julu. Liu Bang's western easy path through Wuguan. Both starting from the same point but different routes to Xianyang.
**Text**: 刘邦为什么能第一个打进咸阳
**Subtitle**: 项羽打硬仗刘邦走捷径

## 02-the-pact
**Visual**: King Huai of Chu making the declaration to assembled generals. The promise: first to enter the pass becomes king. Xiang Yu and Liu Bang both present. The contest begins.
**Text**: 先入关者为王
**Subtitle**: 楚怀王的约定

## 03-xiangyu-stuck
**Visual**: Xiang Yu's forces locked in brutal battle at Julu. Months of fighting. The main Qin army engaged. Zhang Han and Wang Li's forces putting up fierce resistance.
**Text**: 项羽在巨鹿打硬仗消耗数月
**Subtitle**: 赢了但也走不开了

## 04-liubang-west
**Visual**: Liu Bang's army marching west through surrendered cities. Qin officials switching sides. Nanyang surrendering. Easy progress while Xiang Yu fights. Snowball effect.
**Text**: 刘邦走西路一路收编投降
**Subtitle**: 能打就打打不过就绕

## 05-wuguan
**Visual**: Zhang Liang's strategy at Wuguan and Yaoguan. Bribing defenders then surprise attack. The last gates to Xianyang falling. Liu Bang's army entering the pass.
**Text**: 张良献计武关峣关一破
**Subtitle**: 咸阳就在眼前

## 06-surrender
**Visual**: Ziying the Qin emperor in white carriage, holding the imperial seal. Kneeling before Liu Bang. Qin dynasty ends. Liu Bang magnanimous, no killing, no burning.
**Text**: 秦王子婴投降秦朝灭亡
**Subtitle**: 约法三章不杀不烧
