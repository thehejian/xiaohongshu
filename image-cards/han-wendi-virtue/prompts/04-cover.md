Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, abundant negative space, misty atmosphere. Han Emperor Wen Di sitting at his desk, brush in hand, deep in thought. Before him lies an edict inscribed with "废除肉刑" (abolish corporal punishment). Beside it on the floor are the discarded torture instruments — a branding knife, a nose-cutting blade, a foot-amputating axe — scattered on the ground. Candlelight flickers, illuminating the emperor's moved expression. Chinese title: 废除肉刑. Subtitle: 砍脚割鼻刺面的刑罚，从此消失. Ancient Chinese people, Western Han emperor in plain black hempen robe. No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
