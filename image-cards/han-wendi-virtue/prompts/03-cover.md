Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, abundant negative space, misty atmosphere. Outside the Chang'an palace gate, a teenage girl (Chunyu Tiying, about 15) kneels on the stone steps, holding a silk scroll high above her head with both hands, expression determined and sorrowful, tear stains on her face. Her father's prison cart stands in the distance. Emperor Wen's carriage is just leaving the palace, and he sees her from his carriage. Chinese title: 缇萦救父. Subtitle: 一个小姑娘的信，打动了一个皇帝. Ancient Chinese people, Western Han emperor in plain black robe on carriage, teenage girl kneeling in plain clothes. No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
