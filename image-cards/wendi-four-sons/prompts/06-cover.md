Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, abundant negative space, misty atmosphere. A broken bamboo slip suspended in the center of the composition, bearing the original Sima Qian text: "代王王后生四男……及代王立为帝，而王后所生四男更病死." Below the slip, a few drops of ink seep downward like blood. The background is blank rice paper, empty — symbolizing these four boys left no names in history. Chinese title: 史书上的空白. Subtitle: 两千年前司马迁的轻描淡写，每个字底下都是血. Only bamboo slip and ink drops, no human figures. No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
