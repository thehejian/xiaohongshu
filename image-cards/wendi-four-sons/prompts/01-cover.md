Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, abundant negative space, misty atmosphere. Four silhouette figures of young boys standing side by side, faces obscured, slowly dissolving into faint ink mist — symbolizing the four royal sons erased from history. In the foreground, a broken bamboo slip fragment with faint text "代王王后生四男" (the queen of the Prince of Dai bore four sons), the rest of the text covered by ink blot. The composition is ethereal and melancholic, like a grave without a tombstone. Chinese title: 四个被抹去的嫡子. Subtitle: 没有姓名、没有封号、没有陵园. Ancient Chinese figures, only four side silhouette boys. No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
