Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, abundant negative space, misty atmosphere. The Weiyang Palace grand hall at deep night. The silhouette of Emperor Wen Liu Heng sitting alone on the dragon throne, his shadow cast long by candlelight. Outside the hall, four small coffins being carried out of the palace gates by servants moving silently. The emperor does not look back. Two-thirds of the composition is empty palace wall, with only one lonely candle and the corner of a coffin in the lower area. Chinese title: 登基的代价. Subtitle: 入长安那一夜，他杀了四个侄子，又死了四个儿子. Ancient Chinese people, Western Han, Emperor Wen in plain black robe, silhouette only. No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
