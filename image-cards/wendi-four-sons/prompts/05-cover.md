Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, abundant negative space, misty atmosphere. The imperial court, officials bowing heads. Emperor Wen sits upon the throne. A nine-year-old boy (Liu Qi) kneels in the hall, receiving the crown prince investiture ceremony. Dou Yifang (soon to be empress) stands behind a curtain, expression dignified. But in the corner of the composition, four empty small chairs sit with a thin layer of dust — the seats where the four legitimate princes should have been. Chinese title: 立太子. Subtitle: 老五当上了太子，因为前面四个都死了. Ancient Chinese people, Western Han, Emperor Wen in plain black court robe, young Liu Qi in prince robe, Dou Yifang behind curtain in palace dress. No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
