Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. A Han dynasty noblewoman in a flowing cross-collar robe walking through a garden, an embroidered sachet hanging from her waist, soft fragrance wafting. Beside her a maid lifts the lid of a smoking incense stove (xunlong) where a garment airs over the frame, fragrant smoke curling up into the fabric. Petals and spices scattered on a tray. The Han art of fragrant clothing. Chinese title '"香囊·熏衣"' in calligraphic brush style.
