Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. A Han dynasty noblewoman in a crimson cross-collar robe (jiaoling shenyi) sitting before a bronze mirror, applying white lead powder to her face with a puff, her skin ghostly pale. On the dressing table, bowls of white powder, a dish of green mineral pigment (dai) for eyebrows, and a red lip balm jar. In the mirror her reflection shows slender drawn eyebrows. The elaborate Han dynasty beauty routine. Chinese title '"铅华·对镜妆"' in calligraphic brush style.
