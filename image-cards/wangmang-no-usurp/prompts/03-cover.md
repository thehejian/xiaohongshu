Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. A Han dynasty altar with four imperial crowns (冕冠) arranged in a line — each one visibly smaller than the last, the final one tiny as a baby's head. Beside the crowns, a torn family tree scroll with blank spaces where heirs should be. A single jade seal lying broken on the floor. The story of four consecutive Han emperors who died without leaving an adult heir. Chinese title '"四代绝嗣"' in calligraphic brush style.
