Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Wang Mang standing at a crossroads. One road leads to a bright, orderly Han court with the dragon throne — legitimate imperial rule with clear succession. The other road leads to a dark, chaotic scene of samurai warlords fighting under a weak shadow emperor — the Japanese shogun model of constant civil wars and unstable puppet rule. Wang Mang looks toward the imperial throne, rejecting the shogun path with disdain. China's centralized system is strong and stable; Japan's幕府 system is fractured and violent.
