Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Chao Cuo in official court robes is escorted by armored guards to the Eastern Market execution ground. His official hat has fallen to the ground. Bamboo scrolls scatter in the mud. Dark clouds hang low over the sky. In the distance, the palace gates slowly close. Chao Cuo looks back toward the palace, his expression one of sorrow and bewilderment, not understanding his fate. Ancient Chinese people, Han dynasty court and military clothing. Chinese title "朝服腰斩" in calligraphic brush style. Subtitle "吾不爱一人以谢天下". No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
