Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. A small group of Han envoys in Chinese robes riding camels toward a desert oasis city with walled gates, carrying silk bundles and gifts. Date palms and desert landscape. The Loulan city gates open ahead. Chinese title "傅介子·使楼兰" in calligraphic brush style, subtitle "携金锦·入虎穴". No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
