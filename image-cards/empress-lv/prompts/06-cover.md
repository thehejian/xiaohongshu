Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Deep inside the Han palace, Empress Lv lying on the bed, peaceful expression, eyes closed. Palace lantern by the bed about to go out. Outside the hall, faint sounds of weapons and fighting in the darkness — the Lv clan rebellion brewing. Ancient Chinese people, Empress Lv in Han dynasty Empress Dowager clothing. Chinese title 吕后之死 in calligraphic brush style. Subtitle 生前身后，毁誉参半. No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
