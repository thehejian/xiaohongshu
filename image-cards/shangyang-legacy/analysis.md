# Content Analysis: 商鞅是功臣还是罪人

## Content Type
Historical debate / dual-perspective analysis

## Topic
The two sides of Shang Yang's legacy — hero who unified China vs villain who created Legalist tyranny

## Key Points
- Pro: broke aristocratic monopoly, meritocracy, military power, unified China
- Con: harsh punishment, collective responsibility, book burning, culture of fear
- Both perspectives are valid — reflects the contradiction of Legalism itself
- The Warring States context explains the means
- His legacy shaped 2000 years of Chinese governance

## Audience
Chinese history enthusiasts, people interested in the moral evaluation of historical figures
