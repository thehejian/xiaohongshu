Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. A Han dynasty government office scene: an imperial commissioner in embroidered official robes (绣衣直指使者) holding a ceremonial axe (斧钺), reading out a death sentence to kneeling local officials. The condemned officials look terrified. Scrolls and official seals lie on the desk. Tense atmosphere of judgment. Chinese title "沈命·不觉皆死" in calligraphic brush style, subtitle "二千石以下·一律处死". No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
