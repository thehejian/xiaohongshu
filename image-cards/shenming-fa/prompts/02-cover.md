Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. A Han dynasty landscape scene showing armed bandits in ragged clothing setting fire to a government building and breaking into storehouses in a mountain region (泰山). Smoke rises from the burning buildings. Local residents flee through the misty mountain paths. Chaotic but stylized with minimal detail. Chinese title "盗起·泰山" in calligraphic brush style, subtitle "群盗攻城·劫掠府库". No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
