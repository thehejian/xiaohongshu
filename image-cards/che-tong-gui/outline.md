# Outline: 车同轨中央集权的命脉

## 01-cover-tracks
**Visual**: A road dividing into two paths with different track widths. Warring States chariots with different wheel gauges can't cross. Qin decree "车同轨" unifies them into one standard road.
**Text**: 车同轨中央集权的命脉
**Subtitle**: 不是简单的轨道统一

## 02-different-gauges
**Visual**: Map showing different states with different colored tracks. Qi chariot can't enter Zhao, Chu chariot blocked at Qin border. Fragmented transport networks.
**Text**: 战国各国轨距不同
**Subtitle**: 齐国车走不了赵国的路

## 03-imperial-highway
**Visual**: Qin building the imperial highway network. Workers laying roads, trees planted along sides. Roads radiating from Xianyang outward in all directions. Grand infrastructure project.
**Text**: 以咸阳为中心修建驰道
**Subtitle**: 宽五十步路基夯实下雨也能走

## 04-military-speed
**Visual**: Qin army marching swiftly on the standardized road. Chariots and soldiers moving fast. A map showing 7 days to any destination. Military logistics transformed.
**Text**: 军队七天到达任何郡县
**Subtitle**: 中央力量第一次触达每一个角落

## 05-decree-power
**Visual**: Emperor's decree leaving Xianyang palace, traveling along the highway system. Riders on horseback reaching distant provinces. Local officials receiving orders they cannot evade.
**Text**: 政令通畅地方无法推脱
**Subtitle**: 皇帝诏令最短时间传遍天下

## 06-ending-legacy
**Visual**: The unified empire connected by roads. A web of highways binding the realm together. The foundation of Chinese centralization for millennia.
**Text**: 把散沙天下捏成一个帝国
**Subtitle**: 车同轨是中央集权的真正命脉
