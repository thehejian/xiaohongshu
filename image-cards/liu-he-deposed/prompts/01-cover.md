Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. A dramatic scene showing a fleeting reign: a Han dynasty lacquer earcup (耳杯) knocked over on the ground, wine spilling out. In the background, Liu He on horseback racing toward Chang'an, while the imperial throne in the distance fades like a mirage. Scattered bamboo slips suggest his brief 27-day rule. The composition conveys a fleeting, almost farcical reign. Chinese title '“二十七日皇帝”' in calligraphic brush style, subtitle '"速朽之君"'. No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
