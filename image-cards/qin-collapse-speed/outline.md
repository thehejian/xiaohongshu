# Outline

## 01-cover-speed
**Visual**: A towering building built too fast, already cracking at the foundation. Qin dynasty symbol at the top, fractures spreading from the base. Speed vs stability contrast.
**Text**: 秦朝崩溃不是暴政是太快
**Subtitle**: 六国人心未附制度未稳

## 02-conquest-too-fast
**Visual**: Map of China showing Qin conquering six states one after another in just 6 years. Arrows showing the rapid conquest. Too fast to absorb.
**Text**: 六年横扫天下太快了
**Subtitle**: 从灭韩到灭齐势如破竹

## 03-unassimilated
**Visual**: People of conquered states in different regional clothing, looking at Qin rule with resentment. A Qin official trying to enforce laws but meeting resistance.
**Text**: 六国人心未附
**Subtitle**: 楚人想复楚赵人想复赵

## 04-broken-old-order
**Visual**: The old order shattered, new order not yet stable. Traditional local customs vs Qin's unified laws creating tension. Confusion and disorientation.
**Text**: 旧秩序打碎了新秩序没站稳
**Subtitle**: 改革方向对但太急

## 05-sole-reliance
**Visual**: Qin Shi Huang alone holding up the entire empire structure. Everyone else just executing his orders. The system depends entirely on one man.
**Text**: 帝国全靠秦始皇一人撑着
**Subtitle**: 他活着机器转他死了就崩

## 06-ending-revenge
**Visual**: After Qin Shi Huang's death, six states nobles rising up. Xiang Yu, Zhang Liang leading forces. The unconquered old states striking back.
**Text**: 秦朝被来不及消化的六国反噬
**Subtitle**: 不是农民起义是六国复仇
