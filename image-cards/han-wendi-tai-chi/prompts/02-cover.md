The Wei Bridge scene: Emperor Wen Di's carriage stopped mid-bridge. Liu Heng in plain black hempen robe steps down from the carriage, raising his hand to signal "stop." Before him, general Song Chang's hand rests on his sword hilt, eyes sharp, scanning ahead. On the other side of the bridge is the Chang'an city gate, where Zhou Bo and the meritorious officials wait at the entrance, their expressions shifting from enthusiastic welcome to confusion — why has he stopped? The composition creates strong tension: the carriage halted mid-bridge, suggesting the power structure has frozen in place. Chinese title: 渭桥急停. Subtitle: 最想要的东西，偏偏不急着要.
