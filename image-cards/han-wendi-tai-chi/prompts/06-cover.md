Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, abundant negative space, misty atmosphere. Panoramic view of Chang'an city at dusk. Emperor Wen Di stands alone on the city wall, thin silhouette, facing the vast imperial territory, robes billowing in the wind. Above the city wall, faint brush strokes form a barely visible line of text: "没人发现我在出手" (No one realized I was making my move). The setting sun casts a long shadow behind the tower, the entire scene radiating a sense of silent power. Chinese title: 刀过无痕. Subtitle: 最狠的权谋，是不让你发现我在出手.
