Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, abundant negative space, misty atmosphere. In the Weiyang Palace, Emperor Wen Di Liu Heng sits alone playing Go (weiqi), holding a black stone, expression calm and unhurried as he lightly places a piece. In a distant corner of the composition, rendered in extremely faint ink wash, the aftermath of battle — the flags of Jibei rebel forces collapsing. He hasn't even lifted his head to look. Chinese title: 头也不回的平叛. Subtitle: 淡然一句"知道了". Ancient Chinese people, Western Han emperor in plain black hempen robe, alone playing Go. No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
