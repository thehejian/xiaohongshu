Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, abundant negative space, misty atmosphere. Split composition. Top half: Han Emperor Wen Di Liu Heng writing a letter alone by candlelight at night, brush hovering over paper, the letter's opening surprisingly humble, beside it a decree reading "restore the Nanyue king's ancestral tombs." Bottom half: Zhao Tuo, King of Nanyue, reading the letter in his Guangzhou palace, hands trembling, tears streaming down his aged face — conquered by a letter bearing no swords. A faint ink line connects the two halves. Chinese title: 一封信收一国. Subtitle: 不战而屈人之兵的最狠版本. Ancient Chinese people, Western Han emperor in plain black hempen robe, Nanyue king in southern-style royal robe. No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
