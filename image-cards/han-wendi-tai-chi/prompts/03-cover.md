Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, abundant negative space, misty atmosphere. The Weiyang Palace grand hall, air frozen. Zhou Bo kneeling on the ground, holding the imperial jade seal with both hands raised high, forehead nearly touching the floor in a posture of submission. Emperor Wen Di Liu Heng stands on the hall steps, leaning back slightly, one hand slowly gesturing refusal, his face calm as an ancient well. The meritorious officials flanking the hall show varied expressions — some anxious, some confused, some growing uneasy. The scene captures the moment of the FOURTH refusal, everyone in the hall sweating. Chinese title: 五次推让. Subtitle: 每拒绝一次，他们的心就沉一分.
