Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, abundant negative space, misty atmosphere. Han Emperor Wen Di Liu Heng standing by a window in a simple study, back to the viewer showing only a side profile, holding a secret letter in his hand. Outside the window, the emperor's welcoming procession is visible in the distance. His expression is calm, with a faint chilling smile at the corner of his mouth — this Prince of Dai, whom everyone thought was easy to bully, is silently calculating every move in his mind. Ancient Chinese people, Western Han emperor in plain black hempen robe (xuan yi, NO dragon patterns, NO gold). Cold color tone, mysterious atmosphere. Chinese title: 装傻的顶级权谋. Subtitle: 选他，就是看他好欺负. No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
