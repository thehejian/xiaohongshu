Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, vivid crimson and grey colors, dramatic composition, misty atmosphere. A dramatic scene on a lonely moonlit road outside the Han capital: a fleeing messenger in a dark cloak gallops away on a horse at full speed, dust and mist trailing behind him, looking back fearfully as torches flicker in the distance — the imperial pursuit closing in. Behind him, the vast silhouette of Chang'an's city walls and gates loom against the night sky, a pale crescent moon above. The fugitive caught in the web of his own forgery, high tension, dramatic night atmosphere. Chinese title "诈书东窗" in large bold calligraphic brush style.
