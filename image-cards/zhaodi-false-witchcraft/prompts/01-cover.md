Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, vivid crimson and grey colors, dramatic composition, misty atmosphere. A dramatic scene at the towering vermilion gate of Han dynasty Weiyang Palace: a sly, furtive messenger in plain robes hurries up the grand steps, clutching a sealed scroll letter wrapped in black silk to his chest, glancing nervously around while palace guards with halberds stand watch at the gate. Behind him, imposing palace towers rise into mist, banners flutter in the wind. An air of secret intrigue and conspiracy, dramatic tension, shafts of lamplight cutting through dawn mist. Chinese title "诈呈密信" in large bold calligraphic brush style.
