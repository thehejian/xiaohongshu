Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Han dynasty street scene in Chang'an. A young man in rough commoner's clothes in the middle of a cockfight arena, cheering with street friends, betting coins scattered around, his golden-feathered fighting cock facing off against another.
