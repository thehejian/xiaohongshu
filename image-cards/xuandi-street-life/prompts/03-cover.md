Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Han dynasty scene with contrast. Emperor Xuan in full imperial robes sitting on the throne, a slight nostalgic smile on his face, while before his eyes drifts a faint memory of himself as a street urchin fighting cocks and playing cuju in the Chang'an streets. The unlikely journey from street rat to emperor. Chinese title '"天子忆少年"' in calligraphic brush style.
