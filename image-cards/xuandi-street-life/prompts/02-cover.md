Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Han dynasty market scene. A young Liu Bingyi in plain clothes examining a horse at the West Market horse fair, horse merchants gathered around impressed, one suspicious-looking merchant hiding his horse. The keen eye of a streetwise young man who can spot a sick horse or a stolen one at a glance. Chinese title '"市井伯乐"' in calligraphic brush style.
