Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Three scenes in one composition — bottom: young street ruffians fighting cocks and dogs in a Chang'an alley; middle: a wealthy local strongman in a manor courtyard receiving tribute from peasants; top: a powerful 游侠 leader (郭解) in a mansion hall accepting guests and petitioners. Misty transitions between tiers. Chinese title "游侠三级" in calligraphic brush style, subtitle "市井·豪强·京城巨头". No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
