Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. A middle-aged Han dynasty man (郭解) in plain robes listening to a kneeling man who has come to confess a crime, while a woman (郭解's sister) looks on angrily. Courtyard of a modest home, bamboo fence, autumn leaves. The kneeling man bows deeply, head touching the ground. Guō Jiě's expression is calm and forgiving, his hand gesturing the man to rise. Chinese title "郭解·义释" in calligraphic brush style, subtitle "以德报怨·声望愈隆". No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
