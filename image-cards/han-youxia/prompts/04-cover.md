Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. A Han dynasty 游侠 leader seated at a simple wooden table under a large tree, listening to a dispute between two peasants. One peasant gestures pleadingly, the other points accusingly. A few onlookers gathered around. The 游侠's hand rests on a scroll of bamboo slips as if passing judgment. Village scene, thatched roofs visible, late afternoon light. Chinese title "民间·公堂" in calligraphic brush style, subtitle "侠者断事·不请官府". No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
