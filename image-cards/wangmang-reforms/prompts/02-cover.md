Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Han dynasty market scene. A confused merchant holding a handful of bizarre coins — giant ones with holes, tiny shells, tortoise shells, cloth money — five different types all supposedly legal tender. Another merchant arguing, a customer looking hopeless. The chaos of Wang Mang's five currency reforms in ten years. Chinese title '"五改货币·民何以堪"' in calligraphic brush style.
