Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Han dynasty palace under siege. Rebel armies in ragged clothes storming through the palace gates, torches and smoke. Wang Mang in imperial robes running desperately through the corridors, his crown fallen, clutching his jade seal. The tragic end of a utopian dreamer who became a destroyer. Chinese title '"未央终局"' in calligraphic brush style.
