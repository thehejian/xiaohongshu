Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Han dynasty court scene. Wang Mang in imperial robes holding the ancient book Zhouli (Rites of Zhou) high like a sacred text, announcing his land reform decree to bewildered officials who exchange confused glances. The gap between ancient book ideals and practical governance. Chinese title '"王田令·纸上江山"' in calligraphic brush style.
