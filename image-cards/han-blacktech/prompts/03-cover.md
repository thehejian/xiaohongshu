Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Contrast composition: on the left, a Han dynasty soldier in iron armor holding a fine steel longsword; on the right in the distance, a Xiongnu horseman armed with a bone arrow; in the foreground, a farmer plowing deep furrows with an iron plow. The theme: Han power built on iron. Chinese title '"铁器·定天下"' in calligraphic brush style.
