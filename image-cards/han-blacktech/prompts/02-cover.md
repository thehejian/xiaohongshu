Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Han dynasty state-run iron workshop. A tall iron smelting furnace glowing red, an iron official holding a bamboo record scroll supervising, workers using iron tongs to pull glowing iron from the furnace onto an anvil, finished iron ploughs and hoes stacked beside them. Chinese title '"盐铁官营·百炼成钢"' in calligraphic brush style.
