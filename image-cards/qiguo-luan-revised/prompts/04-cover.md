Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. At the Huaishui-Sishui confluence, Marquis Hangu of Gaoyang leads Han light cavalry to cut off Wu-Chu grain boats. Grain ships burn on the river, black smoke billowing. Wu army supply soldiers scatter in panic along the banks. In the distance, the dry supply route and starving Wu-Chu camp are visible. Ancient Chinese people, Han dynasty military clothing. Chinese title "绝其粮道" in calligraphic brush style. Subtitle "断了粮，看你能撑几天". No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
