Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. On the walls of Suiyang, King Liu Wu of Liang in armor personally directs the defense. Liang soldiers fight desperately against the Wu-Chu allied assault. Arrows fly like rain, city bricks crack and crumble. Han Anguo and Zhang Yu stand on either side commanding. The scene is brutal and tragic. Ancient Chinese people, Han dynasty military clothing. Chinese title "睢阳死守" in calligraphic brush style. Subtitle "四十城沦陷，这座城没破". No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
