Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Inside the dark prison of the Tingwei court, Zhou Yafu sits alone on a straw mat, his hair disheveled, his face gaunt and emaciated. Before him, a bowl of rice remains untouched — he has starved himself for five days. A sliver of sky light penetrates through the iron window, illuminating his stubborn, despairing face. The composition is oppressive and bleak. Ancient Chinese man, Han dynasty prison clothing. Chinese title "条侯饿死" in calligraphic brush style. Subtitle "此怏怏者，非少主臣也". No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
