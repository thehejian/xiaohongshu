Ink wash painting style, light rice paper texture, flowing ink strokes, subtle crimson and grey colors, sparse composition with negative space, misty atmosphere. Zhou Yafu stands on the city wall of Changyi, gazing southeast toward Suiyang city where flames shoot skyward and smoke billows — there, King Liu Wu of Liang is fighting to the death. Zhou Yafu's face is expressionless. Behind him, Han troops remain entrenched, motionless. Ancient Chinese people, Han dynasty military clothing. Chinese title "以梁委之" in calligraphic brush style. Subtitle "把梁国扔给吴楚，我去断粮道". No heavy outlines. Ample whitespace.
